        Library file

        The implementation SHALL provide lib_IronAir.slx containing masked first-principles subsystems.
        REQUIREMENT

        SSS002
        SSS002
        Continuous-time ODEs

        Atomic physics blocks SHALL implement continuous first-order ODEs using Simulink Integrator blocks driven by explicit dy/dt equations (no S-Functions), suitable for variable-step stiff solvers (ode15s / ode23tb class).
        REQUIREMENT
        		3
        SSS003
        SSS003
        Electrolyte_1D block

        lib_IronAir SHALL include Electrolyte_1D implementing 1D KOH transport (Fick + migration approximation) and ohmic drop between oxygen-electrode and metal-electrode domains.
        REQUIREMENT
        		4

        SSS004
        Metal_Electrode_1D block

        lib_IronAir SHALL include Metal_Electrode_1D implementing Fe / Fe(OH)2 / Fe3O4 utilization via Faraday's law and Butler-Volmer kinetics.
        REQUIREMENT
        		5
        SSS005
        SSS005
        ORR_Electrode_1D block

        lib_IronAir SHALL include ORR_Electrode_1D for oxygen reduction with O2 mass-transport limitation.
        REQUIREMENT
        		6

        SSS006
        OER_Electrode_1D block

        lib_IronAir SHALL include OER_Electrode_1D for oxygen evolution on a dedicated electrode during charge.
        REQUIREMENT
        		7
        SSS007
        SSS007
        H2_Evolution block

        lib_IronAir SHALL include H2_Evolution computing parasitic HER rate, cumulative H2, and Faradaic efficiency loss.
        REQUIREMENT
        		8
        SSS008
        SSS008
        Thermal_Node_1D block

        lib_IronAir SHALL include Thermal_Node_1D coupling Joule and reaction heat into temperature ODEs that feed Arrhenius kinetics.

        		9
        SSS009
        SSS009
        Cell composition

        Cell_IronAir SHALL compose Electrolyte_1D, Metal_Electrode_1D, ORR_Electrode_1D, OER_Electrode_1D, H2_Evolution, and Thermal_Node_1D via library links.
        REQUIREMENT
        		10
        SSS010
        SSS010
        Stack Module Pack composition

        Stack_IronAir, Module_IronAir, and Pack_IronAir SHALL aggregate lower-level library blocks for series/parallel scaling and bus aggregation only.
        REQUIREMENT
        		11

        SSS011
        Plant schedule block

        Plant_BESS_Schedule SHALL accept exogenous plant power/energy schedules spanning 24-100 h and apply a simple PCS efficiency gain (no switching inverter).

        		12

        SSS012
        DER ride-through monitors

        DER_RideThrough_Monitors SHALL assert IEEE 1547-2018 / UL 1741 style voltage and frequency ride-through envelopes at the plant interface.
        REQUIREMENT
        		13

        SSS013
        Standards assert blocks

        Standards_Assert blocks SHALL monitor UL 9540, NFPA 855, and IEEE 1547.9-2022 envelopes for thermal, H2, SOC, and power quantities where physics exists.

        		14
