    SDD001
    1. Discretization
    UID: SDD001
    STATEMENT:

    Each 1D domain uses Nx control volumes. State vectors are length Nx (or species×Nx). Spatial operators use second-order central differences with Neumann/Dirichlet boundaries as documented in src/ ironair_*_ode.m files.
    SDD002
    2. Cell voltage equation
    UID: SDD002
    STATEMENT:

    Cell voltage is V = E_eq(c,T) - eta_metal - eta_air - I*R_elec where eta_air selects ORR (discharge, I>0 convention discharge positive) or OER (charge).
    SDD003
    3. HER kinetics
    UID: SDD003
    STATEMENT:

    H2 evolution uses Tafel/Butler-Volmer form i_H2 = i0_H2 * exp(alpha_H2*F*eta_H2/(R*T)) gated by metal overpotential; n_dot_H2 = i_H2*A/(2*F); Faradaic efficiency eta_F = 1 - I_H2/(abs(I)+eps).
    SDD004
    4. Solver guidance
    UID: SDD004
    STATEMENT:

    Recommended solvers: ode15s or ode23tb; RelTol 1e-4; AbsTol 1e-6 (concentration) / 1e-3 (temperature). Documented in model ConfigSet of demos.
    SDD005
    5. Multi-rate strategy
    UID: SDD005
    STATEMENT:

    Fast electrical signals (V, I, ride-through) use continuous or small fixed step; thermal uses continuous with larger thermal mass; plant schedule uses slow exogenous From Workspace profiles. Rate Transition blocks separate domains in plant demo.
    SDD006
    6. Mask and requirement citation
    UID: SDD006
    STATEMENT:

    Each masked library block documentation pane SHALL cite relevant SSS/SRS UIDs.
