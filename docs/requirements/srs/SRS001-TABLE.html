        SRS001
        SRS001
        Project identity

        The project SHALL provide a first-principles continuous-time Simulink model of an aqueous alkaline iron-air battery energy storage system (BESS) under the project name iron-air-modeling.

        Establishes system scope and naming for all subordinate requirements.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2
        SRS002
        SRS002
        Chemistry and cell architecture

        The model SHALL represent aqueous alkaline iron-air chemistry with a metal electrode, a dedicated ORR electrode, a dedicated OER electrode, and a static flooded KOH electrolyte domain separating the oxygen electrodes from the metal electrode.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3
        SRS003
        SRS003
        Spatial fidelity

        Electrode and electrolyte domains SHALL be modeled in one spatial dimension (1D) with finite-volume / control-volume discretization.
        REQUIREMENT
        		4
        SRS004
        SRS004
        Hydrogen evolution

        The model SHALL include parasitic hydrogen evolution (HER) on the metal electrode and SHALL expose H2 molar rate, cumulative H2 volume, and Faradaic efficiency loss.
        REQUIREMENT
        		5
        SRS005
        SRS005
        Hierarchical composition

        The system SHALL compose Cell, Stack, Module, Pack, and Plant levels from reusable library components without duplicating physics at higher levels.
        REQUIREMENT
        		6
        SRS006
        SRS006
        Multi-day energy arbitrage

        The plant-level model SHALL support continuous simulation of 24 h to 100 h energy arbitrage power/energy schedules.
        REQUIREMENT
        		7
        SRS007
        SRS007
        Multi-rate timescales

        The system SHALL support electrical, thermal, and multi-day SOC timescales with multi-rate / co-simulation guidance so each component can be simulated and tested independently.
        REQUIREMENT
        		8
        SRS008
        SRS008
        Parameter management

        All model parameters SHALL be defined in lib_IronAir_init.m and stored in the Simulink Data Dictionary lib_IronAir.sldd.
        REQUIREMENT
        		9
        SRS009
        SRS009
        Library packaging

        First-principles components SHALL be packaged in a single Simulink library file lib_IronAir.slx with masks on subsystems where appropriate.
        REQUIREMENT
        		10
        SRS010
        SRS010
        Requirements management

        All project requirements SHALL be managed with StrictDoc under requirements/ using MIL-STD-498 document types (SRS, SSS, SDD, IRS, TP, TD). Doorstop SHALL NOT be used.
        REQUIREMENT
        		11
        SRS011
        SRS011
        IEEE 1547-2018 compliance intent

        The project SHALL document and implement plant-level Simulink monitors consistent with IEEE 1547-2018 interconnection and ride-through behavioral envelopes (voltage and frequency) for DER/ESS plant interface studies. Switching-level inverter models are out of scope.

        Simulink linkage: DER_RideThrough_Monitors block.
        REQUIREMENT
        		12
        SRS012
        SRS012
        IEEE 1547.9-2022 compliance intent

        The project SHALL document ESS interconnection guidance per IEEE 1547.9-2022 as plant interconnection requirements and associated Simulink assertion monitors for energy storage plant studies.
        REQUIREMENT
        		13
        SRS013
        SRS013
        UL 1741 compliance intent

        The project SHALL implement plant-interface behavioral interconnection envelopes consistent with UL 1741 (without PWM/switching PCS detail) via Simulink monitors linked to this requirement.
        REQUIREMENT
        		14
        SRS014
        SRS014
        UL 9540 compliance intent

        The project SHALL implement ESS safety envelope monitors consistent with UL 9540 for thermal limits and abnormal operating conditions exposable by the physics model.
        REQUIREMENT
        		15
        SRS015
        SRS015
        NFPA 855 compliance intent

        The project SHALL implement monitors related to NFPA 855 for thermal and hydrogen accumulation quantities produced by the physics model.
        REQUIREMENT
        		16
        SRS016
        SRS016
        Validation data

        The project SHALL provide estimated synthetic mock cell charge/discharge curves under data/mock/ for regression testing.
        REQUIREMENT
        		17
        SRS017
        SRS017
        Pure Simulink continuous ODEs

        Physics SHALL be implemented as pure Simulink continuous-time ODE systems (no Simscape custom components in v1).
