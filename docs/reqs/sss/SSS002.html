    SEC-SSS2-ELECTRODES

    1. Iron electrode, hydrogen evolution, and dual air electrodes

    UID: SEC-SSS2-ELECTRODES
    STATEMENT: Each DynamicComponent in this document is the software image of a patented plant
    part. Iron, ORR, OER, electrolyte, separator, collector, cell, stack, module,
    and enclosure geometries are taken from US12308414B2 (Claims 1-18 and FIGS. 1-93)
    and EP4602674A1 (Claims 1-89 and FIGS. 1-27). Balance-of-plant electrical and
    thermal components remain required to close the LODES plant of US12308414B2
    FIGS. 94-102.
    IA-FE-001
    1.1. Iron electrode ODE
    UID: IA-FE-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-003 ODE-based components
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.iron_electrode SHALL provide class IronElectrode.

    The component SHALL dynamically model metallic iron, Fe(OH)2, Fe3O4, active surface
    area, passivation thickness, porosity, electrode temperature, and irreversible
    active-material loss.

    The RHS SHALL calculate iron phase derivatives from the shared reaction network.

    The iron electrode SHALL support DRI pellet beds (US12308414B2 Claim 18), porous
    particle assemblies, and channeled rib-and-spine electrodes (EP4602674A1 Claim 1)
    with areal loading 1-7 g Fe/cm2 and electrolyte volume fraction 0.5-0.9 when
    fully charged (EP4602674A1 Claim 16-17).

    Channel length, width, spacing, shape, slope, channel fraction, and spine/rib
    porosity SHALL match IA-SYS-016 and IA-SYS-018. Position relative to ORR and OER
    SHALL be changeable (US12308414B2 Claim 13).
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Claim 18 and FIG. 3 packed DRI bed; EP4602674A1 Claims 1, 16-17 and
    [0051]-[0061]. Thick electrodes need channels to keep HER from dominating charge.
    COMMENT: Discharge of Fe increases Fe(OH)2 using IA-CHM-002 stoichiometry. Temperature is a
    dynamic state. Passivation thickness is nonnegative.
    IA-HER-001
    1.2. Hydrogen evolution ODE
    UID: IA-HER-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-020 Iron-air reaction set
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.hydrogen SHALL provide class HydrogenEvolution.

    States SHALL include hydrogen inventory and applicable interfacial or gas-transfer dynamics.

    The component SHALL couple hydrogen generation to charging current and electrode potential.

    Vertical channels SHALL provide a bubble-egress path for HER (EP4602674A1 [0056]).
    Coulombic efficiency SHALL decrease when ionic path length to the back of a thick
    iron electrode exceeds the resistance of front-surface HER (EP4602674A1 [0047]).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 [0044]-[0047], [0056]. Enclosure ventilation and lid venting in
    US12308414B2 remove evolved hydrogen from the vessel headspace.
    COMMENT: H2 inventory increases under negative iron overpotential. Open-circuit HER is smaller
    than strong cathodic charging HER.
    IA-ORR-001
    1.3. Oxygen reduction ODE
    UID: IA-ORR-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-012 ORR geometry and inverse air-breathing options
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.oxygen_reduction SHALL provide class OxygenReduction.

    States SHALL include interfacial charge storage and oxygen availability where applicable.

    The component SHALL calculate nonlinear ORR kinetics and oxygen consumption.

    ORR SHALL be configurable as floating, vertical natural-air-breathing, inverse
    air-breathing, tubular, stacked submerged sections with depth-equalized pressure
    drop, wavy/rippled, or bifunctional, per IA-SYS-009 and IA-SYS-012
    (US12308414B2 Claims 1, 4, 7-12).

    One ORR face SHALL face electrolyte or channels and the opposing face SHALL
    contact air (EP4602674A1 Claim 1). Hydrophobic GDE layers with PTFE/carbon MPL
    examples from US12308414B2 SHALL be parameterizable.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Claims 1, 4, 7-12 and cathode construction; EP4602674A1 Claim 1
    air-facing second surface.
    COMMENT: ORR current is negative (cathodic) for eta < 0 versus the ORR Nernst potential.
    Oxygen consumption matches Faraday's law for four-electron ORR.
    IA-OER-001
    1.4. Oxygen evolution ODE
    UID: IA-OER-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-015 Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.oxygen_evolution SHALL provide class OxygenEvolution.

    The component SHALL calculate oxygen evolution, catalyst activity, and applicable
    dynamic electrode states.

    OER SHALL support planar, submerged, interdigitated trunk-and-projection,
    corrugated, serpentine, discrete cathode arrays, spiral bifilar, and pleated
    layouts (EP4602674A1 Claims 1, 3, 12, 32, 38, 70, 77).

    Default OER construction SHALL be porous metal mesh plus oxygen-evolution
    catalyst (Claim 11). OER SHALL sit closer to the iron electrode than ORR during
    charge and SHALL be electrically isolatable from ORR (US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 Claims 1, 3, 11-12, 38, 70, 77; US12308414B2 dual-cathode toggle.
    COMMENT: OER current is positive (anodic) for eta > 0 versus the OER Nernst potential.
    IA-GDL-001
    1.5. Gas diffusion ODE
    UID: IA-GDL-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-014 Airflow regulation and ORR separator
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.gas_diffusion SHALL provide class GasDiffusionLayer.

    The component SHALL calculate transient oxygen concentration profiles, effective
    diffusion, pore saturation, flooding, and gas/liquid transport.

    Hydraulic head on vertical ORR SHALL be able to drive liquid migration and
    flooding; OER bubbles trapped at a horizontal air electrode SHALL be able to
    dry the triple-phase boundary (US12308414B2 vertical versus horizontal
    architecture discussion, FIGS. 8-9B). Stacked submerged ORR SHALL capture
    cascading bubbles with baffles, lips, or increasing pocket thickness
    (FIGS. 25A-36B, Claims 1 and 14-15).
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 flooding, dry-out, bubble-trap, and stacked-core air utilization.
    pO2 window 0.01-100 atm discharge and 0.001-100 atm charge.
    COMMENT: A step decrease in boundary O2 mole fraction produces a delayed interior O2 decline.
    Flooding (saturation -> 1) reduces effective O2 diffusivity.
    IA-ELY-001
    1.6. Electrolyte ODE
    UID: IA-ELY-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-021 Electrolyte composition and pH domain
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.electrolyte SHALL provide class Electrolyte.

    States SHALL include water inventory, dissolved species, electrolyte temperature,
    carbonation, and spatial concentration distributions where enabled.

    The model SHALL include concentration-dependent KOH physical properties.

    Default composition SHALL be 6 M aqueous KOH. Alternate patented mixtures from
    IA-SYS-021 SHALL be selectable. Carbonation from CO2-bearing air SHALL consume
    OH- and MAY precipitate solids that clog pores (US12308414B2). High hydroxide
    (7 M or greater) SHALL be allowed as a capacity and coulombic-efficiency lever
    (EP4602674A1).
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 electrolyte examples and CO2 discussion; EP4602674A1 Claims 14-15
    and high-hydroxide paragraph.
    COMMENT: Conductivity is computed from instantaneous KOH concentration and temperature via
    ironair.properties. Carbonate accumulation consumes OH-.
    IA-SEP-001
    1.7. Separator ODE
    UID: IA-SEP-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-025 Current collectors, separators, and seals
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.separator SHALL provide class Separator.

    The model SHALL represent transient species transport, electrolyte saturation where
    applicable, degradation, and separator temperature.

    Ionic resistance SHALL be calculated from the instantaneous physical state.

    The separator SHALL be able to block dissolved oxygen and oxygen bubbles from
    reaching the iron electrode without a substantial ionic-resistance penalty, while
    remaining hydrophilic and macroporous (US12308414B2 FIG. 4A). It MAY be a
    compression frame, porous insulator, ribbed bubble-egress structure, or porous
    dielectric coating on iron and/or OER (EP4602674A1 [0048], Claim 2). Wrapped
    stacked-core separators MAY use 1 micrometer to 1 centimeter pores
    (US12308414B2 FIG. 36A).
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 oxygen-blocking separator; EP4602674A1 Claim 2 and [0048].
    COMMENT: R_ionic is algebraic in conductivity, thickness, area, and saturation. Temperature
    is dynamic.
    IA-COL-001
    1.8. Current collector ODE
    UID: IA-COL-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-025 Current collectors, separators, and seals
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.current_collector SHALL provide class CurrentCollector.

    The model SHALL include thermal dynamics and configurable long-term contact or
    resistance degradation.

    Ohmic voltage loss SHALL be calculated algebraically.

    Collectors SHALL include anode branch-plus-primary layouts, ORR dual-face tabs,
    Ni-coated-steel ORR collectors with EPDM gasket compression
    (US12308414B2 FIGS. 4B-4C), stainless-steel wires/sheets or mesh cages in iron
    ribs (EP4602674A1), and can-negative housings (US12308414B2 Claim 5).
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Claims 5 and 17 and FIGS. 4B-4C; EP4602674A1 Claims 13, 30-31.
    COMMENT: No fake lag state for resistance. V_ohm = I * R(T, degradation_state).
    IA-CEL-001
    1.9. Complete cell ODE
    UID: IA-CEL-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-008 Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.cell SHALL provide class IronAirCell.

    The cell SHALL couple iron electrode, air electrode, hydrogen evolution, electrolyte,
    separator, gas diffusion, current collectors, and thermal dynamics.

    The cell SHALL calculate instantaneous terminal voltage, current, power, SOC, and
    available capacity.

    The component SHALL solve coupled nonlinear electrochemical and electrical constraints.

    Charge SHALL close iron to OER to power source; discharge SHALL close iron to ORR
    to load (EP4602674A1 [0049]). Independent charge and discharge length scales
    SHALL be parameterizable (US12308414B2 Lc versus Ld, including 100 h / 300 h
    asymmetric example). Shared-electrolyte baths of multiple cells SHALL be allowed
    (US12308414B2 FIG. 9A vessel 970).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 dual-electrode switch; US12308414B2 dual-cathode and duration scaling.
    COMMENT: Cell rhs solves algebraic current/voltage consistency. Outputs include V, I, P, SOC.
    SEC-SSS2-BOP

    2. Thermal, air, and electrolyte hydraulic plant

    UID: SEC-SSS2-BOP
    IA-THM-001
    2.1. Thermal ODE
    UID: IA-THM-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-011 Stackable vessel and secondary containment
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.thermal SHALL provide class ThermalNetwork.

    States SHALL include thermal energy or temperature at relevant physical nodes.

    The model SHALL calculate conduction, convection, radiation, reaction heat, and cooling.

    Vessel cavity flow (air or liquid), thermal-mass water fill, and lid-level thermal
    management from US12308414B2 FIGS. 1E-1F and 9A SHALL map onto ThermalNetwork
    nodes. Wide/low vessels SHALL be usable to increase surface area for heat
    rejection.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 double-wall cavity 161 and multi-function lid thermal management.
    COMMENT: Adiabatic isolated node with positive heat source increases temperature.
    IA-AIR-001
    2.2. Air handling ODE
    UID: IA-AIR-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-014 Airflow regulation and ORR separator
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-AIR-002 Fan ODE
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.air_system SHALL provide class AirSystem.

    The component SHALL include gas inventories, manifold pressure dynamics, oxygen
    concentration, humidity, and airflow coupling.

    Air delivery SHALL include natural air-breathing, forced supply, sparging/bubbling
    into submerged ORR, cascading stacked-core flow, snorkel/test-tube mixed-phase
    channels, and ramp arrays with holes (US12308414B2 Claims 9, 14-15; FIGS. 37-51,
    71A-71B). Outlet pressure regulation SHALL be able to hold unsealed air-chamber
    pressure.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 air-supply and stacked-core architectures. Equal pressure drop across
    depth-stacked ORR sections is Claim 1.
    COMMENT: Manifold pressure rises when inlet mass flow exceeds outlet plus consumption.
    IA-AIR-002

    STATUS: Active

    SEC-SSS2-PLANT

    3. Stack, module, enclosure, and electrical conversion

    UID: SEC-SSS2-PLANT
    IA-STK-001
    3.1. Stack ODE
    UID: IA-STK-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-011 Stackable vessel and secondary containment
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.stack SHALL provide class BatteryStack.

    The component SHALL aggregate cell states while preserving cell-level variations and
    electrical current conservation.

    Stacked vessels with conductive contact points SHALL be able to place cells in
    series (US12308414B2 Claim 3). Arrays of coaxial ORR-core / hexagonal-anode cells
    in a shared electrolyte pool (FIGS. 27-34) and multi-cell housings with dividing
    walls (FIG. 13) SHALL be supported. Double-sided and air-channel-paired cells
    from EP4602674A1 Claims 18-19 SHALL be stackable.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Claims 3 and 5, FIGS. 13 and 27-34; EP4602674A1 Claims 18-19.
    COMMENT: Series stack current is identical through cells. Terminal voltage is the sum of cell voltages.

    STATUS: Active

    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.enclosure SHALL provide class BatteryEnclosure.

    The model SHALL include enclosure temperature, ventilation, auxiliary electrical loads,

    The enclosure SHALL implement stackable-vessel, double-wall secondary containment,

    COMMENT: Ventilation flow reduces enclosure H2 inventory. Auxiliary load is an algebraic electrical output.

    IA-GRD-001
    3.8. Grid interface ODE
    UID: IA-GRD-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-007 Long-duration energy storage mission
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.grid SHALL provide class GridInterface.

    The grid SHALL support dynamic voltage and frequency disturbances, network interface
    equations, and configurable external-grid dynamic models.

    A prescribed grid voltage or frequency SHALL be treated as an external input, not an
    invented dynamic state.

    Grid-connected LODES dispatch of surplus solar over seasonal windows
    (US12308414B2 Definitions; FIGS. 94-102 bulk energy storage systems) SHALL be
    representable as GridInterface scenarios.
    COMMENT: When voltage/frequency are prescribed, they appear in inputs. Optional swing-equation
    mode is explicitly identified.
    IA-DEG-001
    3.9. Degradation ODE
    UID: IA-DEG-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-023 Water, carbonation, and hydrogen management
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.degradation SHALL provide class Degradation.

    The model SHALL represent irreversible material changes, catalyst decay, resistance
    growth, and active capacity loss.

    ORR damage from high OER potential, PTFE hydrophobicity loss, carbonate pore
    clogging, salt crusting on air electrodes, and iron passivation SHALL be
    representable aging modes (US12308414B2 dual-cathode isolation, CO2, and crusting
    discussion; EP4602674A1 passivation via Fe(OH)2 films). Seasonal anode replacement
    SHALL be an allowed discrete maintenance event (US12308414B2 replaceable electrodes).
    COMMENT: Degradation states are nondecreasing under positive aging rates. Capacity loss is irreversible.
    IA-FLT-001

    UID: IA-FLT-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-011 Stackable vessel and secondary containment
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.components.sensors SHALL provide class DynamicSensor.

    The model SHALL include finite response time, sensor drift, bias, and noise.

    Lid-mounted reactor controls and sensing of US12308414B2 FIG. 9A SHALL map onto
    DynamicSensor instances for voltage, current, temperature, electrolyte level, gas
    flow, and hydrogen.
    COMMENT: First-order sensor lag is a dynamic state. Noise uses a documented random seed.
    IA-CTL-001
    3.11. Supervisory control dynamics
    UID: IA-CTL-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-008 Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.controls.supervisory SHALL provide class SupervisoryController.

    The controller SHALL include explicit continuous controller states where applicable,
    as well as discrete operating modes.

    It SHALL be implemented as a hybrid system with ODE dynamics and discrete transitions.

    Discrete modes SHALL include charge (iron-OER-source), discharge (iron-ORR-load),
    rest, protection, and optional airflow-flex isolation of ORR from OER
    (EP4602674A1 switch 116; US12308414B2 switch 550 and Claim 16).
    COMMENT: Mode transitions are event-driven. PI or equivalent continuous states exist for current/power regulation.
    IA-EST-001
    3.12. State estimation
    UID: IA-EST-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-IRS-005 Estimator measurement-only interface
    STATEMENT: Module ironair.controls.estimation SHALL provide class BatteryStateEstimator.

    The model SHALL estimate SOC, SOH, active iron inventory, and available charge/discharge power.

    It SHALL not access the simulated plant's true hidden states during observer execution.
    COMMENT: Estimator rhs and update methods accept only measurements and previous estimates,
    never plant.y hidden components.
