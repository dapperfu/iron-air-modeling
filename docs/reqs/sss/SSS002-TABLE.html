        IA-FE-001
        Iron electrode ODE
        Module ironair.components.iron_electrode SHALL provide class IronElectrode.

        The component SHALL dynamically model metallic iron, Fe(OH)2, Fe3O4, active surface
        area, passivation thickness, porosity, electrode temperature, and irreversible
        active-material loss.

        The RHS SHALL calculate iron phase derivatives from the shared reaction network.
        Discharge of Fe increases Fe(OH)2 using IA-CHM-002 stoichiometry. Temperature is a
        dynamic state. Passivation thickness is nonnegative.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2
        IA-HER-001
        Active
        IA-HER-001
        Hydrogen evolution ODE
        Module ironair.components.hydrogen SHALL provide class HydrogenEvolution.

        States SHALL include hydrogen inventory and applicable interfacial or gas-transfer dynamics.

        The component SHALL couple hydrogen generation to charging current and electrode potential.
        H2 inventory increases under negative iron overpotential. Open-circuit HER is smaller
        than strong cathodic charging HER.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3
        IA-ORR-001
        Active
        IA-ORR-001
        Oxygen reduction ODE
        Module ironair.components.oxygen_reduction SHALL provide class OxygenReduction.

        States SHALL include interfacial charge storage and oxygen availability where applicable.

        The component SHALL calculate nonlinear ORR kinetics and oxygen consumption.
        ORR current is negative (cathodic) for eta < 0 versus the ORR Nernst potential.
        Oxygen consumption matches Faraday's law for four-electron ORR.
        REQUIREMENT
        		4
        IA-OER-001
        Active
        IA-OER-001
        Oxygen evolution ODE
        Module ironair.components.oxygen_evolution SHALL provide class OxygenEvolution.

        The component SHALL calculate oxygen evolution, catalyst activity, and applicable
        dynamic electrode states.
        OER current is positive (anodic) for eta > 0 versus the OER Nernst potential.
        REQUIREMENT
        		5
        IA-GDL-001
        Active
        IA-GDL-001
        Gas diffusion ODE
        Module ironair.components.gas_diffusion SHALL provide class GasDiffusionLayer.

        The component SHALL calculate transient oxygen concentration profiles, effective
        diffusion, pore saturation, flooding, and gas/liquid transport.
        A step decrease in boundary O2 mole fraction produces a delayed interior O2 decline.
        Flooding (saturation -> 1) reduces effective O2 diffusivity.
        REQUIREMENT
        		6
        IA-ELY-001
        Active
        IA-ELY-001
        Electrolyte ODE
        Module ironair.components.electrolyte SHALL provide class Electrolyte.

        States SHALL include water inventory, dissolved species, electrolyte temperature,
        carbonation, and spatial concentration distributions where enabled.

        The model SHALL include concentration-dependent KOH physical properties.
        Conductivity is computed from instantaneous KOH concentration and temperature via
        ironair.properties. Carbonate accumulation consumes OH-.
        REQUIREMENT
        		7
        IA-SEP-001
        Active
        IA-SEP-001
        Separator ODE
        Module ironair.components.separator SHALL provide class Separator.

        The model SHALL represent transient species transport, electrolyte saturation where
        applicable, degradation, and separator temperature.

        Ionic resistance SHALL be calculated from the instantaneous physical state.
        R_ionic is algebraic in conductivity, thickness, area, and saturation. Temperature
        is dynamic.
        REQUIREMENT
        		8
        IA-COL-001
        Active
        IA-COL-001
        Current collector ODE
        Module ironair.components.current_collector SHALL provide class CurrentCollector.

        The model SHALL include thermal dynamics and configurable long-term contact or
        resistance degradation.

        Ohmic voltage loss SHALL be calculated algebraically.
        No fake lag state for resistance. V_ohm = I * R(T, degradation_state).
        REQUIREMENT
        		9
        IA-CEL-001
        Active
        IA-CEL-001
        Complete cell ODE
        Module ironair.components.cell SHALL provide class IronAirCell.

        The cell SHALL couple iron electrode, air electrode, hydrogen evolution, electrolyte,
        separator, gas diffusion, current collectors, and thermal dynamics.

        The cell SHALL calculate instantaneous terminal voltage, current, power, SOC, and
        available capacity.

        The component SHALL solve coupled nonlinear electrochemical and electrical constraints.
        Cell rhs solves algebraic current/voltage consistency. Outputs include V, I, P, SOC.
        REQUIREMENT
        		10
        IA-THM-001
        Active
        IA-THM-001
        Thermal ODE
        Module ironair.components.thermal SHALL provide class ThermalNetwork.

        States SHALL include thermal energy or temperature at relevant physical nodes.

        The model SHALL calculate conduction, convection, radiation, reaction heat, and cooling.
        Adiabatic isolated node with positive heat source increases temperature.
        REQUIREMENT
        		11
        IA-AIR-001
        Active
        IA-AIR-001
        Air handling ODE
        Module ironair.components.air_system SHALL provide class AirSystem.

        The component SHALL include gas inventories, manifold pressure dynamics, oxygen
        concentration, humidity, and airflow coupling.
        Manifold pressure rises when inlet mass flow exceeds outlet plus consumption.
        REQUIREMENT
        		12

        IA-AIR-002
        Fan ODE
        Module ironair.components.fan SHALL provide class Fan.

        The model SHALL include rotor speed dynamics and fan power consumption.
        Rotor speed is a dynamic state. Electrical power is an algebraic output from speed and torque.
        REQUIREMENT
        		13

        IA-FLD-001
        Electrolyte circulation ODE
        Module ironair.components.electrolyte_flow SHALL provide class ElectrolyteFlow.

        The component SHALL dynamically track flow, species transfer, pressure or hydraulic
        storage where physically justified, and temperature.
        Species transport delay appears when using the detailed hydraulic mode.
        REQUIREMENT
        		14
        IA-FLD-002
        Active
        IA-FLD-002
        Pump ODE
        Module ironair.components.pump SHALL provide class Pump.

        The component SHALL model motor and hydraulic response, electrical input, and mechanical losses.
        Shaft speed is dynamic. Hydraulic power is less than electrical input by documented efficiency.
        REQUIREMENT
        		15
        IA-FLD-003
        Active
        IA-FLD-003
        Reservoir ODE
        Module ironair.components.reservoir SHALL provide class Reservoir.

        The component SHALL model liquid inventory, chemical species inventories, temperature,
        and boundary flows.
        Mass balance: dM/dt equals inlet minus outlet minus leak.
        REQUIREMENT
        		16
        IA-FLD-004
        Active
        IA-FLD-004
        Valve ODE
        Module ironair.components.valve SHALL provide class Valve.

        The component SHALL model actuator response, valve position, and flow-dependent
        hydraulic characteristics.
        Position is a dynamic actuator state. Flow is algebraic in position and pressure drop.
        REQUIREMENT
        		17
        IA-FLD-005
        Active
        IA-FLD-005
        Piping ODE
        Module ironair.components.piping SHALL provide class Pipe.

        The component SHALL model transient fluid transport and temperature or species
        residence-time dynamics when using the detailed hydraulic mode.
        Detailed mode has at least one transport delay state. Lumped mode is flagged reduced_order.
        REQUIREMENT
        		18
        IA-HEX-001
        Active
        IA-HEX-001
        Heat exchanger ODE
        Module ironair.components.heat_exchanger SHALL provide class HeatExchanger.

        The component SHALL represent thermal storage and dynamic heat transfer between fluid circuits.
        Two fluid temperatures approach each other under heat transfer with no external loss.
        REQUIREMENT
        		19
        IA-STK-001
        Active
        IA-STK-001
        Stack ODE
        Module ironair.components.stack SHALL provide class BatteryStack.

        The component SHALL aggregate cell states while preserving cell-level variations and
        electrical current conservation.
        Series stack current is identical through cells. Terminal voltage is the sum of cell voltages.
        REQUIREMENT
        		20
        IA-MOD-001
        Active
        IA-MOD-001
        Module ODE
        Module ironair.components.module SHALL provide class BatteryModule.

        The model SHALL include coupled stacks, shared fluid equipment, air equipment, and thermal states.
        Module assembles at least one stack plus shared thermal or fluid nodes.
        REQUIREMENT
        		21
        IA-ENC-001
        Active
        IA-ENC-001
        Enclosure ODE
        Module ironair.components.enclosure SHALL provide class BatteryEnclosure.

        The model SHALL include enclosure temperature, ventilation, auxiliary electrical loads,
        and enclosure-level gas inventories.
        Ventilation flow reduces enclosure H2 inventory. Auxiliary load is an algebraic electrical output.
        REQUIREMENT
        		22

        IA-DC-001
        DC bus ODE
        Module ironair.components.dc_bus SHALL provide class DCBus.

        The component SHALL model DC link capacitor voltage and stored energy.
        C * V * dV/dt equals net electrical power into the capacitor.
        REQUIREMENT
        		23

        IA-CNV-001
        DC/DC converter ODE
        Module ironair.components.dc_converter SHALL provide class DCDCConverter.

        The model SHALL include averaged inductor current, capacitor voltage, and converter losses.
        Averaged inductor current is a dynamic state. Losses increase output current demand.
        REQUIREMENT
        		24
        IA-INV-001
        Active
        IA-INV-001
        Inverter ODE
        Module ironair.components.inverter SHALL provide class GridInverter.

        The model SHALL include averaged electrical control dynamics, DC link interaction,
        and active/reactive power response.
        P and Q track references through explicit controller states, not prescribed algebraic jumps only.
        REQUIREMENT
        		25
        IA-TRF-001
        Active
        IA-TRF-001
        Transformer ODE
        Module ironair.components.transformer SHALL provide class Transformer.

        The component SHALL include electromagnetic dynamic states where detailed transient
        modeling is enabled, along with winding and core thermal dynamics.
        Detailed mode includes flux or current states. Thermal states exist in both modes.
        Prescribed grid voltage is an input, not a transformer state.
        REQUIREMENT
        		26
        IA-GRD-001
        Active
        IA-GRD-001
        Grid interface ODE
        Module ironair.components.grid SHALL provide class GridInterface.

        The grid SHALL support dynamic voltage and frequency disturbances, network interface
        equations, and configurable external-grid dynamic models.

        A prescribed grid voltage or frequency SHALL be treated as an external input, not an
        invented dynamic state.
        When voltage/frequency are prescribed, they appear in inputs. Optional swing-equation
        mode is explicitly identified.
        REQUIREMENT
        		27
        IA-DEG-001
        Active
        IA-DEG-001
        Degradation ODE
        Module ironair.components.degradation SHALL provide class Degradation.

        The model SHALL represent irreversible material changes, catalyst decay, resistance
        growth, and active capacity loss.
        Degradation states are nondecreasing under positive aging rates. Capacity loss is irreversible.
        REQUIREMENT
        		28
        IA-FLT-001
        Active
        IA-FLT-001
        Sensor ODE
        Module ironair.components.sensors SHALL provide class DynamicSensor.

        The model SHALL include finite response time, sensor drift, bias, and noise.
        First-order sensor lag is a dynamic state. Noise uses a documented random seed.
        REQUIREMENT
        		29
        IA-CTL-001
        Active
        IA-CTL-001
        Supervisory control dynamics
        Module ironair.controls.supervisory SHALL provide class SupervisoryController.

        The controller SHALL include explicit continuous controller states where applicable,
        as well as discrete operating modes.

        It SHALL be implemented as a hybrid system with ODE dynamics and discrete transitions.
        Mode transitions are event-driven. PI or equivalent continuous states exist for current/power regulation.
        REQUIREMENT
        		30

        IA-EST-001
        State estimation
        Module ironair.controls.estimation SHALL provide class BatteryStateEstimator.

        The model SHALL estimate SOC, SOH, active iron inventory, and available charge/discharge power.

        It SHALL not access the simulated plant's true hidden states during observer execution.
        Estimator rhs and update methods accept only measurements and previous estimates,
        never plant.y hidden components.
