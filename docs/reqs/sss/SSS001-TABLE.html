        SEC-SSS1-REQ
        Requirements management
        TEXT
        This document specifies foundation, chemistry, ODE, notebook, and verification
        requirements for ironair. Chemical stoichiometry, capacities, electrolyte
        compositions, and kinetics domains are taken from US12308414B2 and EP4602674A1.
        Software tests verify implementation against these statements; they do not
        validate the patents experimentally.
        REQUIREMENT
        		1.1
        IA-REQ-001
        Active
        Parents:

        IA-REQ-001
        StrictDoc source of truth
        All requirements SHALL be maintained in StrictDoc .sdoc files.

        Markdown SHALL be used for supplementary explanatory design documents but SHALL NOT
        replace the authoritative requirements database.
        Every IA-* UID exists in reqs/**/*.sdoc. CI fails if a source @relation UID is missing
        from the StrictDoc database.

        SEC-SSS1-CON
        Constants, parameters, and property models
        TEXT
        US12308414B2 defines room temperature as 25 C and standard temperature and
        pressure as 25 C and 1 atm, with "about" meaning plus or minus 10 percent or
        instrument error, whichever is larger. EP4602674A1 simulations use 6 M KOH at
        303 K. Property models SHALL cover those reference states.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.1
        IA-CON-001
        Active
        Children:

        IA-CON-001
        Constants module
        Implement src/ironair/constants.py providing F_C_MOL, R_J_MOL_K, T_REF_K, P_REF_PA,
        M_FE_KG_MOL, M_O2_KG_MOL, M_H2O_KG_MOL, and M_KOH_KG_MOL.

        T_REF_K SHALL default to 298.15 K (25 C) and P_REF_PA to 101325 Pa (1 atm) to
        match US12308414B2 standard ambient temperature and pressure. A 303 K property
        set SHALL be available for EP4602674A1 simulation conditions.

        Fundamental constants SHALL be immutable.
        from ironair import constants succeeds. Named constants match CODATA/IUPAC values
        within documented precision. Assignment to module constants raises an error or is
        rejected by typing/runtime freeze.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.2
        IA-CON-002
        Active
        IA-CON-002
        Parameter definitions
        Every component SHALL use explicit, typed parameter objects.

        Parameters SHALL include name, value, SI unit, physical meaning, valid range,
        source classification, literature reference when available, uncertainty when available,
        and calibration status.

        Parameter categories SHALL distinguish fundamental constant, literature value,
        experimental measurement, engineering design assumption, calibrated coefficient,
        and unverified placeholder.

        Patent-disclosed values (electrolyte molarity, channel lengths, iron loading,
        pO2 windows, theoretical mAh/g) SHALL be tagged as literature value with the
        patent number and claim or paragraph in the literature-reference field. They
        SHALL NOT be tagged as experimental measurement unless an independent hardware
        campaign is recorded.
        PhysicalParameter dataclass enforces required fields and rejects out-of-range values.
        Unverified placeholders are queryable via calibration_status.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.3
        IA-CON-003
        Active
        Parents:

        IA-CON-003
        Property models
        Implement concentration- and temperature-dependent properties for KOH conductivity,
        KOH density, KOH viscosity, KOH heat capacity, KOH water activity, dissolved oxygen
        solubility, oxygen diffusivity, gas-phase diffusivity, iron conductivity, thermal
        conductivity, reaction rate constants, and electrochemical exchange-current densities.

        Property models SHALL enforce their valid domains.

        KOH molarity domain SHALL include the patented compositions 5 M to 11 M hydroxide,
        including 6 M KOH, 5.5 M KOH + 0.5 M LiOH, 6 M NaOH, 5 M NaOH + 1 M KOH, and
        7 M or greater hydroxide (US12308414B2 electrolyte examples; EP4602674A1
        [0231]-[0241]).
        Property functions raise a domain error outside documented T and concentration ranges
        and return finite SI values inside the range.

        Shared iron-air chemistry (US12308414B2 and EP4602674A1)
        TEXT
        EP4602674A1 Equations 1-2 and US12308414B2 air-electrode half-reactions are the

        REQUIREMENT

        IA-CHM-001
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
        IA-CHM-001
        Stoichiometric reaction network
        Implement a common chemical reaction network.

        Mathematical model:

        dn/dt = S @ r + B @ q

        Where S is the chemical stoichiometric matrix, r is the reaction-rate vector,
        B maps boundary flows to species inventories, and q is the boundary-flow vector.

        The reaction network SHALL enforce elemental conservation.
        For every reaction column, elemental composition @ S[:, j] is numerically zero.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3.2
        IA-CHM-002
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-020 Iron-air reaction set
        IA-CHM-002
        Iron conversion
        Implement Fe + 2OH- <-> Fe(OH)2 + 2e- as EP4602674A1 [0042] Equation 1.

        Calculate reaction rates from electrode kinetics and Faraday's law.

        Theoretical first-step capacity SHALL be 960 mAh per gram of metallic Fe
        (EP4602674A1 [0043]). Net volume increase on discharge SHALL be accommodated in
        anode porosity rather than by creating unphysical inventory.
        EP4602674A1 [0042]-[0043]. US12308414B2 identifies Fe and Fe-alloys (FeAl, FeZn,
        FeMg) as metal-electrode options; iron is the default LODES anode.
        Two electrons per iron atom. Faraday mapping r = i * A / (n * F) holds within solver tolerance.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3.3
        IA-CHM-003
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-020 Iron-air reaction set
        IA-CHM-003
        Magnetite formation
        Implement configurable conversion between iron hydroxide and magnetite, including
        electron transfer and correct stoichiometry.

        The second discharge step SHALL correspond to further oxidation of Fe(OH)2 to
        Fe3O4 with theoretical capacity 320 mAh per gram of Fe (EP4602674A1 [0043]
        Equation 2). Charge SHALL reduce higher-valence iron species toward Fe(0)
        (EP4602674A1 [0049]).
        EP4602674A1 [0042]-[0043], [0049]. Magnetite is the example higher-valence product
        on discharge through the dual-electrode load path.
        Default electrochemical path conserves Fe, O, H, and charge. A Schikorr chemical path
        may be enabled without inventing extra electrons.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3.4

        IA-CHM-004
        Oxygen reduction reaction
        Implement O2 + 2H2O + 4e- -> 4OH- as the ORR discharge reaction of
        US12308414B2 (four-electron oxygen reduction at the gas-solid-electrolyte
        triple-phase boundary).

        ORR SHALL consume gaseous oxygen typically sourced from air and SHALL require
        triple-phase contact among O2, an electronically conductive solid, and
        electrolyte.
        US12308414B2 detailed description of cathode 120 / GDE operation. EP4602674A1
        FIG. 1 ORR electrode 104 is the discharge positive.
        Four electrons per O2. OH- production matches Faraday's law.
        REQUIREMENT

        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-020 Iron-air reaction set
        IA-CHM-005
        Oxygen evolution reaction
        Implement 4OH- -> O2 + 2H2O + 4e- as the OER charge reaction on a dedicated
        OER electrode (US12308414B2 dual cathode; EP4602674A1 [0048]-[0049]).

        OER kinetics, catalyst state, and bubble generation SHALL be independent of ORR
        so that high OER potentials do not oxidize the ORR electrode when the electrodes
        are electrically separated (US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
        Dual-electrode configuration in both patents. EP4602674A1 Claim 11: OER as porous
        metal mesh plus oxygen-evolution catalyst (Ni, Ni-Fe, Mn oxide, Fe, NiOx,
        NiOx(OH)y, FeOx, FeOx(OH)y).
        OER is the reverse four-electron oxygen couple of ORR with independent kinetics.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3.6
        IA-CHM-006
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-020 Iron-air reaction set
        IA-CHM-006
        Hydrogen evolution reaction
        Implement 2H2O + 2e- -> H2 + 2OH- as the parasitic hydrogen evolution reaction
        that competes with iron recharge (EP4602674A1 [0044], [0047]).

        HER SHALL be favored at the electrode front when ionic path length to unreacted
        oxidized iron at the back is large. Channeled and interdigitated geometries
        SHALL reduce that path length and SHALL be able to increase coulombic efficiency
        relative to planar electrodes (EP4602674A1 FIGS. 19-21).
        EP4602674A1 [0044]-[0050] and FIGS. 19-26. Water as HER reactant can be replenished
        while iron hydroxide at the back remains transport-limited.
        Two electrons per H2. Rate increases with more negative iron overpotential.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3.7

        Children:
        • IA-TD-004 Butler-Volmer equilibrium test
        IA-CHM-007
        Butler-Volmer kinetics
        Implement nonlinear electrode kinetics with exchange-current density, transfer coefficients,
        local overpotential, temperature, species activities, electrode surface area, and
        reaction availability.

        Exchange-current densities SHALL be temperature- and concentration-dependent
        within the patented alkaline domain. Interdigitated OER SHALL be allowed to
        lower effective ionic overpotential relative to planar OER at the same loading
        (EP4602674A1 FIGS. 19-24).
        Zero overpotential yields zero net Faradaic current at unit activities. Current is
        odd in overpotential for symmetric coefficients.
        REQUIREMENT
        		3.8

        IA-CHM-008
        Nernst potentials
        Calculate equilibrium potentials using thermodynamically consistent activities and
        standard electrode-potential references.
        Nernst potentials shift (RT/nF) ln(activity quotient) relative to documented E0.
        REQUIREMENT

        IA-CHM-009
        Reaction thermodynamics
        Calculate Gibbs free energy, reaction enthalpy, reaction entropy, equilibrium cell
        potential, reversible reaction heat, and irreversible reaction heat.
        E_eq = -DeltaG / (n F). Irreversible heat is eta * i * A. Reversible heat uses T * DeltaS.
        REQUIREMENT

        IA-CHM-011
        Coulombic efficiency versus electrode architecture
        The chemistry-to-electrode coupling SHALL report instantaneous coulombic charge
        efficiency as the fraction of charge current stored in intended iron reduction
        products rather than HER (EP4602674A1 [0044], FIGS. 19, 21, 23, 25).

        Planar, channeled, and interdigitated OER/iron layouts SHALL be comparable on
        the same ParameterSet. Interdigitated layouts SHALL be able to show higher
        coulombic efficiency and lower sensitivity of efficiency to areal loading than
        planar layouts, matching the qualitative simulation results of EP4602674A1
        FIGS. 19-24 at 6 M KOH, 303 K, initial rib porosity 0.6, example ChanFrac 0.4,
        w_rib 5 mm, and example loading 4 g Fe/cm2.
        EP4602674A1 [0261]-[0280] and FIGS. 19-27. FIG. 27 experimental interdigitated
        cell showed an extended upper discharge voltage plateau versus planar control
        at matched loading, temperature, current, electrolyte, and ORR.

        IA-ODE-001
        Common component protocol
        Every dynamic component SHALL implement rhs(self, t, y, inputs, context).

        The RHS SHALL be deterministic for a fixed state, time, input, and parameter configuration.

        It SHALL NOT modify component state through hidden side effects.
        Repeated rhs evaluations with identical arguments return identical arrays. Component
        parameter objects are unchanged after rhs.
