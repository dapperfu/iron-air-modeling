    1. Python package interfaces

     SEC-IRS-PY
    • IA-SYS-001
      1.1. Python package IA-SYS-001 The software SHALL be implemented as an installable Python package named ironair.

      All public APIs SHALL be importable using:

      import ironair
      from ironair import constants
      from ironair import chemistry
      from ironair import components
      from ironair import simulation
      from ironair import scenarios
      from ironair import plotting

      The implementation SHALL use a standard src/ package layout.

      The project SHALL provide editable installation using:

      pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]"

    • IA-ODE-001
      4.1. Common component protocol IA-ODE-001 Every dynamic component SHALL implement rhs(self, t, y, inputs, context).

      The RHS SHALL be deterministic for a fixed state, time, input, and parameter configuration.

      It SHALL NOT modify component state through hidden side effects.
      • IA-SYS-003
        7.1. ODE-based components IA-SYS-003 Every physical component SHALL expose a dynamic ODE model.

        Each component SHALL explicitly define: state vector, state units, initial
        conditions, input variables, output variables, governing differential equations,
        algebraic constitutive relations, physical parameters, boundary conditions,
        conservation residuals, numerical constraints, and associated StrictDoc
        requirement IDs.

        Every component SHALL provide a rhs() method returning state derivatives.

        The generic mathematical representation SHALL be:

        dx/dt = f(t, x, inputs, parameters)

        Where required:

        0 = g(t, x, algebraic_variables, inputs, parameters)

        Algebraic variables SHALL be solved consistently during RHS evaluation or through
        a supported DAE backend.

        An artificial time constant SHALL NOT be introduced solely to make an instantaneous
        physical relation appear dynamic.

        Passive structures, such as current collectors, SHALL possess actual dynamic states
        where justified, such as temperature. Their instantaneous electrical resistance
        SHALL remain an algebraic relation.

        Patent-derived algebraic laws SHALL include Ohmic drop, hydraulic orifice and
        equalized stacked-ORR pressure drop, Nernst potential, and Faraday mapping of
        current to reaction rate.
    IA-IRS-002
    1.2. RHS signature IA-IRS-002 Every dynamic component SHALL expose:

    def rhs(self, t, y, inputs, context) -> numpy.ndarray

    t is time in seconds, y is a 1-D float64 state vector, inputs is a mapping of named
    physical inputs, and context carries shared couplings without hidden mutation of
    component parameters.
    • IA-ODE-003
      4.3. Coupled solver IA-ODE-003 Implement ironair.simulation.CoupledSystem.

      The solver SHALL assemble component states into one global state vector, maintain a
      state-index map, resolve shared inputs and algebraic couplings, evaluate the coupled
      RHS, pass the assembled system to SciPy, detect numerical failures, and return state
      trajectories and diagnostics.
    IA-IRS-003
    1.3. CoupledSystem simulate interface IA-IRS-003 ironair.simulation.CoupledSystem.simulate SHALL accept t_span, optional t_eval,
    optional inputs callable, solver method, and tolerances, and SHALL return a
    SimulationResult.

    t_span SHALL accept LODES windows including 8 h, 100 h (3.6e5 s), 300 h, and
    multi-day values from US12308414B2 without changing parameter objects.
    • IA-NBK-003
      5.3. Shared plotting API IA-NBK-003 Implement reusable plotting functions under ironair.plotting.

      Plotting functions SHALL accept structured simulation results or pandas DataFrames.
    IA-IRS-004
    1.4. Plotting input types IA-IRS-004 ironair.plotting functions SHALL accept SimulationResult or pandas.DataFrame.

    Plot metadata SHALL be able to carry scenario identifier, patent source citation,
    and operating assumptions (electrolyte molarity, temperature, architecture mode).
    • IA-EST-001
      3.12. State estimation IA-EST-001 Module ironair.controls.estimation SHALL provide class BatteryStateEstimator.

      The model SHALL estimate SOC, SOH, active iron inventory, and available charge/discharge power.

      It SHALL not access the simulated plant's true hidden states during observer execution.
    IA-IRS-005
    1.5. Estimator measurement-only interface IA-IRS-005 BatteryStateEstimator SHALL accept measurements and prior estimates only. It SHALL
    NOT receive the plant hidden state vector during observer execution.

    • IA-SYS-014
      3.6. Airflow regulation and ORR separator IA-SYS-014 The air system SHALL include airflow regulation structures (US12308414B2 Claim 14),
      including arrays of ramps configured with holes (Claim 15).

      The ORR electrode SHALL optionally include a porous separator (Claim 17), with
      pore sizes in the 1 micrometer to 1 centimeter range when wrapping stacked-core
      ORR columns (FIG. 36A-36B) to retain gas and limit anode contact with oxygen.

      OER and ORR SHALL be electrically separable by presence or absence of airflow that
      flexes and displaces the ORR electrode (Claim 16).

        Vessel, lid, and air-delivery functions disclosed in US12308414B2 (electrolyte
        containment, secondary containment, thermal management cavity, air distribution,
        process-gas venting, current collection, sensing, DC connections) SHALL map onto
        the enclosure, air-system, thermal, collector, and control components listed
        above.
    IA-IRS-006
    2.1. Air interface IA-IRS-006 AirSystem and OxygenReduction SHALL exchange named flows for dry air or oxygen,
    humidity, CO2 mole fraction, pO2, volumetric or mass flow, and manifold pressure.

    ORR SHALL expose an electrolyte-facing first surface and an air-facing second
    surface (EP4602674A1 Claim 1). Stacked submerged ORR sections SHALL expose per-
    section pressure drop so that Claim 1 equal-dP can be enforced.

    Airflow regulation structures including holed ramp arrays (US12308414B2 Claims 14-15)
    SHALL appear as named hydraulic parameters, not as undocumented magic numbers.
    • IA-SYS-021
      5.2. Electrolyte composition and pH domain IA-SYS-021 The default liquid electrolyte SHALL be an aqueous alkaline hydroxide solution.

      The model SHALL accept compositions disclosed in the patents, including:

      - 6 M KOH (US12308414B2; EP4602674A1 simulation electrolyte at 303 K);
      - 5.5 M KOH + 0.5 M LiOH;
      - 6 M NaOH;
      - 5 M NaOH + 1 M KOH;
      - about 4 M KOH + 2 M NaOH + 0.05 M LiOH, or 4 M NaOH + 2 M KOH + 0.05 M LiOH;
      - high hydroxide at or above 5 M, including 5-6 M, 6-7 M, 7-11 M, 7-10 M,
      7.5-9.5 M, and 7 M or greater (EP4602674A1 electrolyte section).

      pH SHALL be modelable as acidic (pH < 4), near-neutral (4 < pH < 10), or alkaline
      (pH > 10), with alkaline pH greater than 10 or greater than 12 as preferred
      operating points (US12308414B2 electrolyte paragraph).

      Electrolyte type MAY also be solid oxide, solid polymer, molten salt, non-aqueous,
      gel, or combinations (EP4602674A1 Claims 14-15), but aqueous alkali hydroxide is
      the verified modeling default.

    2.2. Electrolyte and hydraulic interface IA-IRS-007 Electrolyte, Reservoir, Pump, Valve, and Pipe SHALL exchange water, KOH/NaOH/LiOH,
    carbonate, dissolved oxygen, liquid volume, temperature, and leak flows.

    Primary-to-secondary containment leak into vessel cavity 161 (US12308414B2
    FIGS. 1E-1F) SHALL be a named leak path. Shared electrolyte baths of multiple
    cells SHALL use a common electrolyte context key.
    • IA-SYS-025
      6.2. Current collectors, separators, and seals IA-SYS-025 Anode current collectors SHALL support branch collectors parallel to channels plus
      a primary collector or bus bar parallel to the ORR first surface, including bus
      bars outside the active-area plane (EP4602674A1 Claims 13, 30-31, 47-48).

      Branch collectors SHALL be modelable as highly conductive wires or sheets
      (example stainless steel) inside ribs, or as mesh/cage compressing each rib or
      anode.

      ORR tab current collectors MAY be placed on both faces of the ORR electrode.
      ORR seals MAY sandwich a compliant EPDM gasket between a rigid electrode and a
      Ni-coated-steel current collector under compressive load (US12308414B2 FIGS. 4B-4C).

      Separators SHALL block dissolved oxygen and oxygen bubbles from the anode without
      unduly raising ionic resistance, remaining hydrophilic and macroporous
      (US12308414B2 FIG. 4A). Separators MAY be compression frames, porous insulators,
      ribbed bubble-egress structures, or porous dielectric coatings (EP4602674A1
      [0048], Claim 2).

    IA-IRS-008
    2.3. Electrical tab and stacking interface IA-IRS-008 Iron, ORR, and OER components SHALL expose named electrical ports for anode tabs,
    OER cathode tabs, ORR tabs, can-negative housing, and stacking contact points
    (US12308414B2 Claims 3 and 5; EP4602674A1 FIG. 8 tabs 230, 240a, 240b).

    Charge and discharge paths SHALL be distinct named connections selectable by
    SupervisoryController. Bus bars outside the active-area plane (EP4602674A1
    Claims 30-31) SHALL be identifiable in the state-index map.
    • IA-SYS-016
      4.2. Channel and OER projection geometry IA-SYS-016 Channel and OER geometry parameters SHALL include the ranges disclosed in
      EP4602674A1:

      - channel average length 3 mm to 50 mm perpendicular to the ORR plane, with
      nested examples 15-35 mm, 15-30 mm, and 15-25 mm (Claim 7; [0053]);
      - channel average width 1 mm to 40 mm parallel to the ORR plane, with nested
      examples 2-30 mm, 2-20 mm, and 2-15 mm (Claims 7-8; [0054]);
      - adjacent-channel center spacing 10 mm to 50 mm, with nested examples 10-30 mm
      and 10-20 mm (Claim 9; [0055]);
      - channel shapes independently rectangular prism, cylindrical, pyramidal, or
      trapezoidal prism (Claim 10);
      - sloped channels whose width nearest the ORR is 1 percent to 500 percent greater
      than the width farthest from the ORR (Claim 61; FIG. 5);
      - OER cathode projections of average length 3 mm to 50 mm from a trunk, with
      nested examples 5-35 mm and 10-30 mm, and width 0.1-20 mm or 0.5-15 mm
      (Claims 3-4; [0064]-[0065]);
      - greater than 25 percent of channels occupied by OER, with optional greater than
      40, 50, or 60 percent (Claim 5; [0066]);
      - channel fraction about 0.1 to 0.5, example 0.2 to 0.45, defined as
      CW/(CW + RW); example ChanFrac 0.4 with w_rib 5 mm ([0247], FIGS. 25-26);
      - iron-to-OER gap 0.1 mm to 5 mm, example 1-4 mm ([0260]).
      • IA-SYS-015
        4.1. Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER IA-SYS-015 The system SHALL model an iron electrode in contact with an anode current collector
        and comprising a plurality of channels; an ORR electrode with a first surface
        facing the channels and an opposing second surface in contact with air; an OER
        electrode interdigitated with the channels, with at least a portion of the OER
        disposed within the channels in a direction perpendicular to the ORR plane; and
        electrolyte in contact with the iron electrode, the ORR first surface, the
        channels, and the OER electrode.

        This configuration SHALL be the default representation of EP4602674A1 Claim 1
        (FIG. 2).

    IA-IRS-009
    2.4. Channel and interdigitation interface IA-IRS-009 IronElectrode SHALL publish channel occupancy, ChanFrac, rib width, and whether
    each channel contains OER, additive, both, or neither (EP4602674A1 Claims 5-6).

    OxygenEvolution SHALL publish projection length, trunk presence, corrugated versus
    discrete-cathode layout, and iron-OER gap. Separator SHALL publish ionic
    resistance and bubble-egress availability between those ports.
    • IA-SYS-006
      1.2. Patent-grounded plant of record IA-SYS-006 The simulated plant of record SHALL be a rechargeable aqueous alkaline iron-air
      battery with a dual-electrode air side (distinct ORR discharge cathode and OER
      charge cathode), an iron-containing metal electrode, and a liquid electrolyte that
      separates the air-side electrodes from the metal electrode.

      The plant SHALL be consistent with US12308414B2 Claim 1 (ORR, OER, metal electrode,
      and electrolyte) and with EP4602674A1 Claim 1 (iron electrode, ORR electrode with
      air-facing surface, interdigitated OER, and contacting electrolyte).

      Primary-only (ORR-only air electrode) operation MAY be enabled as a documented
      reduced-order mode but SHALL be identified as non-rechargeable relative to the
      secondary dual-electrode plant.

    IA-IRS-010
    2.5. Scenario and architecture selection interface IA-IRS-010 ironair.scenarios SHALL accept an architecture identifier covering US12308414B2
    Claim 1 stacked ORR, Claim 18 DRI bed, floating ORR, and EP4602674A1 Claims 1, 20,
    38, 55, 70, and 77 layouts.

    Invalid combinations (for example bifunctional ORR forced to OER potential while
    isolation is required) SHALL raise a documented configuration error.
