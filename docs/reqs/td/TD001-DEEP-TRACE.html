    • IA-SYS-001
      1.1. Python package IA-SYS-001 The software SHALL be implemented as an installable Python package named ironair.

      All public APIs SHALL be importable using:

      import ironair
      from ironair import constants
      from ironair import chemistry
      from ironair import components
      from ironair import simulation
      from ironair import scenarios
      from ironair import plotting

      The implementation SHALL use a standard src/ package layout.

      The project SHALL provide editable installation using:

      pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]"

    • IA-CON-001
      2.1. Constants module IA-CON-001 Implement src/ironair/constants.py providing F_C_MOL, R_J_MOL_K, T_REF_K, P_REF_PA,

      Fundamental constants SHALL be immutable.

      reaction availability.

    2. Component, traceability, and patent-architecture tests

     SEC-TD-ARCH
    • IA-ODE-001
      4.1. Common component protocol IA-ODE-001 Every dynamic component SHALL implement rhs(self, t, y, inputs, context).

      The RHS SHALL be deterministic for a fixed state, time, input, and parameter configuration.

      It SHALL NOT modify component state through hidden side effects.
      • IA-SYS-003
        7.1. ODE-based components IA-SYS-003 Every physical component SHALL expose a dynamic ODE model.

        Each component SHALL explicitly define: state vector, state units, initial
        conditions, input variables, output variables, governing differential equations,
        algebraic constitutive relations, physical parameters, boundary conditions,
        conservation residuals, numerical constraints, and associated StrictDoc
        requirement IDs.

        Every component SHALL provide a rhs() method returning state derivatives.

        The generic mathematical representation SHALL be:

        dx/dt = f(t, x, inputs, parameters)

        Where required:

        0 = g(t, x, algebraic_variables, inputs, parameters)

        Algebraic variables SHALL be solved consistently during RHS evaluation or through
        a supported DAE backend.

        An artificial time constant SHALL NOT be introduced solely to make an instantaneous
        physical relation appear dynamic.

        Passive structures, such as current collectors, SHALL possess actual dynamic states
        where justified, such as temperature. Their instantaneous electrical resistance
        SHALL remain an algebraic relation.

        Patent-derived algebraic laws SHALL include Ohmic drop, hydraulic orifice and
        equalized stacked-ORR pressure drop, Nernst potential, and Faraday mapping of
        current to reaction rate.
    IA-TD-005

    • IA-SYS-008
      2.3. Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths IA-SYS-008 During charge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the OER
      electrode and a power source so that iron species are reduced toward metallic
      Fe(0).

      During discharge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the
      ORR electrode and a load so that metallic iron is oxidized toward higher-valence
      species including Fe(OH)2 and Fe3O4.

      A mode switch SHALL select charge versus discharge electrical paths as in
      EP4602674A1 [0049] switch 116 and US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B toggle switch 550.

      OER and ORR electrodes SHALL be allowed to be electrically separated so that the
      ORR electrode is not exposed to high OER operating potentials that can damage or
      oxidize the ORR electrode (US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
      • IA-SYS-002
        2.2. Integrated physics simulation IA-SYS-002 The software SHALL model the complete iron-air battery as a coupled multiphysics
        system corresponding to the patented plant.

        The model SHALL include metallic iron electrode, iron hydroxide and oxide phases,
        oxygen reduction electrode, oxygen evolution electrode, hydrogen evolution,
        alkaline electrolyte, electrolyte transport, separator, gas diffusion layer,
        current collectors, cell assembly, thermal system, air intake, fan/blower, gas
        manifolds, ventilation, electrolyte reservoir, circulation pump, piping, valves,
        heat exchanger, battery stack, battery module, battery enclosure, DC electrical
        distribution, DC/DC conversion, grid inverter, transformer, grid interface,
        measurement sensors, battery management, supervisory control, fault management,
        state estimation, and degradation and aging.

        These components SHALL interact through shared physical quantities and conserved
        material, charge, and energy flows.

        Vessel, lid, and air-delivery functions disclosed in US12308414B2 (electrolyte
        containment, secondary containment, thermal management cavity, air distribution,
        process-gas venting, current collection, sensing, DC connections) SHALL map onto
        the enclosure, air-system, thermal, collector, and control components listed
        above.

    • IA-CHM-011
      3.11. Coulombic efficiency versus electrode architecture IA-CHM-011 The chemistry-to-electrode coupling SHALL report instantaneous coulombic charge
      efficiency as the fraction of charge current stored in intended iron reduction
      products rather than HER (EP4602674A1 [0044], FIGS. 19, 21, 23, 25).

      Planar, channeled, and interdigitated OER/iron layouts SHALL be comparable on
      the same ParameterSet. Interdigitated layouts SHALL be able to show higher
      coulombic efficiency and lower sensitivity of efficiency to areal loading than
      planar layouts, matching the qualitative simulation results of EP4602674A1
      FIGS. 19-24 at 6 M KOH, 303 K, initial rib porosity 0.6, example ChanFrac 0.4,
      w_rib 5 mm, and example loading 4 g Fe/cm2.
      • IA-SYS-015
        4.1. Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER IA-SYS-015 The system SHALL model an iron electrode in contact with an anode current collector

        electrode interdigitated with the channels, with at least a portion of the OER
        disposed within the channels in a direction perpendicular to the ORR plane; and
        electrolyte in contact with the iron electrode, the ORR first surface, the
        channels, and the OER electrode.

        This configuration SHALL be the default representation of EP4602674A1 Claim 1
        (FIG. 2).

    2.5. Interdigitated coulombic-efficiency trend test IA-TD-009 Run matched planar, channeled, and interdigitated layouts at 6 M KOH, 303 K,

    • IA-SYS-021
      5.2. Electrolyte composition and pH domain IA-SYS-021 The default liquid electrolyte SHALL be an aqueous alkaline hydroxide solution.

      The model SHALL accept compositions disclosed in the patents, including:

      - 6 M KOH (US12308414B2; EP4602674A1 simulation electrolyte at 303 K);
      - 5.5 M KOH + 0.5 M LiOH;
      - 6 M NaOH;
      - 5 M NaOH + 1 M KOH;
      - about 4 M KOH + 2 M NaOH + 0.05 M LiOH, or 4 M NaOH + 2 M KOH + 0.05 M LiOH;
      - high hydroxide at or above 5 M, including 5-6 M, 6-7 M, 7-11 M, 7-10 M,
      7.5-9.5 M, and 7 M or greater (EP4602674A1 electrolyte section).

      pH SHALL be modelable as acidic (pH < 4), near-neutral (4 < pH < 10), or alkaline
      (pH > 10), with alkaline pH greater than 10 or greater than 12 as preferred
      operating points (US12308414B2 electrolyte paragraph).

      Electrolyte type MAY also be solid oxide, solid polymer, molten salt, non-aqueous,
      gel, or combinations (EP4602674A1 Claims 14-15), but aqueous alkali hydroxide is
      the verified modeling default.
