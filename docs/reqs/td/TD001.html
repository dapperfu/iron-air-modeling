    SEC-TD-FOUND

    1. Foundation and chemistry tests

    UID: SEC-TD-FOUND
    STATEMENT: These test descriptions specify how phase-gate and patent-mapped requirements are
    executed. Procedures operate on the ironair package. They do not energize physical
    cells from US12308414B2 or EP4602674A1.
    IA-TD-001
    1.1. Package import test
    UID: IA-TD-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-001 Python package
    STATEMENT: Import ironair and the public subpackages listed in IA-SYS-001.
    COMMENT: No ImportError. __version__ is a nonempty string.
    IA-TD-002
    1.2. Constants immutability test
    UID: IA-TD-002
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-CON-001 Constants module
    STATEMENT: Read F_C_MOL, R_J_MOL_K, T_REF_K, P_REF_PA, M_FE_KG_MOL, M_O2_KG_MOL, M_H2O_KG_MOL,
    and M_KOH_KG_MOL. Attempting assignment shall fail.

    Confirm T_REF_K corresponds to 25 C and P_REF_PA to 1 atm per US12308414B2
    standard ambient temperature and pressure.
    COMMENT: Values match documented CODATA/IUPAC constants. Frozen assignment raises.
    IA-TD-003
    1.3. Elemental conservation test
    UID: IA-TD-003
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-TST-001 Elemental conservation
    STATEMENT: For each column of S, compute the elemental residual E @ S[:, j] for Fe, O, H, K,
    and C, including Fe/Fe(OH)2, Fe(OH)2/Fe3O4, ORR, OER, and HER columns from
    IA-SYS-020.
    COMMENT: Absolute residual < 1e-12 for Fe, O, H, K, and C.
    IA-TD-004
    1.4. Butler-Volmer equilibrium test
    UID: IA-TD-004
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-CHM-007 Butler-Volmer kinetics
    STATEMENT: Evaluate electrode current at zero overpotential with finite activities for iron,
    ORR, OER, and HER couples.
    COMMENT: Net Faradaic current is ~0. Symmetric coefficients produce an odd i(eta) function.
    SEC-TD-ARCH

    2. Component, traceability, and patent-architecture tests

    UID: SEC-TD-ARCH
    IA-TD-005
    2.1. RHS determinism test
    UID: IA-TD-005
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-ODE-001 Common component protocol
    STATEMENT: Evaluate rhs twice with identical arguments for every DynamicComponent, including
    IronElectrode in DRI-bed and channeled modes, OxygenReduction in stacked submerged
    mode, and OxygenEvolution in interdigitated mode.
    COMMENT: Arrays compare equal. Component parameter values are unchanged.
    IA-TD-006
    2.2. Traceability test
    UID: IA-TD-006
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-REQ-004 Bidirectional traceability
    STATEMENT: Parse reqs/**/*.sdoc UIDs and src/**/*.py @relation markers.

    Patent-mapped UIDs IA-SYS-006 through IA-SYS-026, IA-CHM-011, IA-SDD-006 through
    IA-SDD-010, IA-IRS-006 through IA-IRS-010, IA-TP-004 through IA-TP-006,
    IA-TD-007 through IA-TD-010, and IA-SCN-PAT-* SHALL exist in StrictDoc.
    COMMENT: Every @relation UID exists. Every implementation requirement listed in the
    traceability map has a function marker.
    IA-TD-007
    2.3. Dual-electrode switch test
    UID: IA-TD-007
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-008 Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths
    STATEMENT: Place IronAirCell in charge mode and confirm the electrical path is iron-OER-source
    with ORR isolated from OER potential. Switch to discharge and confirm iron-ORR-load
    (EP4602674A1 [0049]; US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
    COMMENT: Mode is discrete. ORR is not held at OER potential during charge when isolation
    is enabled.
    IA-TD-008
    2.4. Stacked ORR equal pressure-drop test
    UID: IA-TD-008
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-009 Submerged stacked ORR with depth-equalized pressure drop
    STATEMENT: Build a vertical stack of discrete submerged ORR sections with depth-varying
    geometry. Compute algebraic pressure drop in the depth direction
    (US12308414B2 Claim 1).
    COMMENT: Pressure drops compare equal within solver tolerance. Geometry may still vary
    with depth.
    IA-TD-009
    2.5. Interdigitated coulombic-efficiency trend test
    UID: IA-TD-009
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-CHM-011 Coulombic efficiency versus electrode architecture
    STATEMENT: Run matched planar, channeled, and interdigitated layouts at 6 M KOH, 303 K,
    ChanFrac 0.4, w_rib 5 mm, and 4 g Fe/cm2 (EP4602674A1 FIGS. 19-27 conditions).
    Compare instantaneous coulombic charge efficiency and discharge voltage plateau
    length.
    COMMENT: Interdigitated efficiency is greater than or equal to planar efficiency at
    end-of-charge. Test records architecture flags and does not claim hardware
    validation.
    IA-TD-010
    2.6. Electrolyte and loading domain test
    UID: IA-TD-010
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-021 Electrolyte composition and pH domain
    STATEMENT: Construct ParameterSets for 6 M KOH, 5.5 M KOH + 0.5 M LiOH, 6 M NaOH, 5 M NaOH +
    1 M KOH, and 7 M hydroxide, and for iron loading 1 and 7 g Fe/cm2 with electrolyte
    volume fraction 0.5 and 0.9 (US12308414B2 electrolyte examples; EP4602674A1
    Claims 16-17). Reject a loading of 0 g Fe/cm2 or a volume fraction of 1.1.
    COMMENT: In-range sets construct. Out-of-range sets raise a domain error.
