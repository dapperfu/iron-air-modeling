    1. Foundation and chemistry tests

     UID: SEC-TD-FOUND
    These test descriptions specify how phase-gate and patent-mapped requirements are
    executed. Procedures operate on the ironair package. They do not energize physical
    cells from US12308414B2 or EP4602674A1.
    • IA-SYS-001
      1.1. Python package UID: IA-SYS-001 STATUS: Active RELATIONS (Child):
      • IA-IRS-001 Public package imports
      • IA-SDD-001 Source layout
      • IA-SDD-005 Clean Python baseline
      • IA-REQ-001 StrictDoc source of truth
      • IA-TD-001 Package import test
      STATEMENT: The software SHALL be implemented as an installable Python package named ironair.

      All public APIs SHALL be importable using:

      import ironair
      from ironair import constants
      from ironair import chemistry
      from ironair import components
      from ironair import simulation
      from ironair import scenarios
      from ironair import plotting

      The implementation SHALL use a standard src/ package layout.

      The project SHALL provide editable installation using:

      pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]"
       RATIONALE: A single installable package is the integration boundary for notebooks, tests, and
      coupled simulation of the patented iron-air plant. The SDRS src/ layout takes
      precedence over the generic no-src-directory Cursor rule for this project.
       COMMENT: Package ironair installs in a virtual environment. The listed imports succeed after
      pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]" or the equivalent uv installation.

    • IA-CHM-007
      3.7. Butler-Volmer kinetics UID: IA-CHM-007 STATUS: Active RELATIONS (Child):
      • IA-TD-004 Butler-Volmer equilibrium test
      STATEMENT: Implement nonlinear electrode kinetics with exchange-current density, transfer coefficients,
      local overpotential, temperature, species activities, electrode surface area, and
      reaction availability.

      Exchange-current densities SHALL be temperature- and concentration-dependent
      within the patented alkaline domain. Interdigitated OER SHALL be allowed to
      lower effective ionic overpotential relative to planar OER at the same loading
      (EP4602674A1 FIGS. 19-24).
       COMMENT: Zero overpotential yields zero net Faradaic current at unit activities. Current is
      odd in overpotential for symmetric coefficients.

     COMMENT: Net Faradaic current is ~0. Symmetric coefficients produce an odd i(eta) function.

    • IA-SYS-008
      2.3. Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths UID: IA-SYS-008 STATUS: Active RELATIONS (Parent):
      • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • IA-SDD-006 Dual-electrode electrical design
      • IA-SYS-010 Separate ORR and OER electrodes
      • IA-CEL-001 Complete cell ODE
      • IA-CTL-001 Supervisory control dynamics
      • IA-TD-007 Dual-electrode switch test
      STATEMENT: During charge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the OER
      electrode and a power source so that iron species are reduced toward metallic
      Fe(0).

      During discharge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the
      ORR electrode and a load so that metallic iron is oxidized toward higher-valence
      species including Fe(OH)2 and Fe3O4.

      A mode switch SHALL select charge versus discharge electrical paths as in
      EP4602674A1 [0049] switch 116 and US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B toggle switch 550.

      OER and ORR electrodes SHALL be allowed to be electrically separated so that the
      ORR electrode is not exposed to high OER operating potentials that can damage or
      oxidize the ORR electrode (US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
       RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 [0048]-[0049] and US12308414B2 dual-cathode architecture. Independent
      OER/ORR materials and geometries are separately optimized (US12308414B2).
       COMMENT: The supervisory controller implements the switch as a discrete operating mode, not
      as a hidden mutation of plant parameters.

    • IA-SYS-009
      3.1. Submerged stacked ORR with depth-equalized pressure drop UID: IA-SYS-009 STATUS: Active RELATIONS (Parent):
      • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • IA-SDD-008 ORR geometric modes
      • IA-SCN-PAT-STACKORR Submerged stacked ORR equal pressure drop
      • IA-TD-008 Stacked ORR equal pressure-drop test
      • IA-TP-005 Architecture verification objectives
      STATEMENT: The system SHALL support a submerged ORR electrode comprising a series of discrete
      ORR electrode sections arranged vertically in a stack below the electrolyte
      surface.

      Each discrete ORR section SHALL have geometry that can vary with depth below the
      electrolyte surface.

      The air-path model SHALL be able to impose equal pressure drop across each stacked
      ORR section in the depth direction.

      This configuration SHALL be the default representation of US12308414B2 Claim 1.
       RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Claim 1. Detailed description FIGS. 25A-36B, 57-58, and 35A-35C
      describe stacked-core / distillation-column ORR layers with depth-dependent bubble
      thickness, baffles, and cascading air.
       COMMENT: Equal pressure drop is an algebraic hydraulic constitutive law, not an artificial
      dynamic lag (IA-SYS-003).

    • IA-CHM-011
      3.11. Coulombic efficiency versus electrode architecture UID: IA-CHM-011 STATUS: Active RELATIONS (Parent):
      • IA-SYS-015 Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • IA-SCN-PAT-INTERDIG Interdigitated OER versus planar control
      • IA-TD-009 Interdigitated coulombic-efficiency trend test
      STATEMENT: The chemistry-to-electrode coupling SHALL report instantaneous coulombic charge
      efficiency as the fraction of charge current stored in intended iron reduction
      products rather than HER (EP4602674A1 [0044], FIGS. 19, 21, 23, 25).

      Planar, channeled, and interdigitated OER/iron layouts SHALL be comparable on
      the same ParameterSet. Interdigitated layouts SHALL be able to show higher
      coulombic efficiency and lower sensitivity of efficiency to areal loading than
      planar layouts, matching the qualitative simulation results of EP4602674A1
      FIGS. 19-24 at 6 M KOH, 303 K, initial rib porosity 0.6, example ChanFrac 0.4,
      w_rib 5 mm, and example loading 4 g Fe/cm2.
       RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 [0261]-[0280] and FIGS. 19-27. FIG. 27 experimental interdigitated
      cell showed an extended upper discharge voltage plateau versus planar control
      at matched loading, temperature, current, electrolyte, and ORR.

    • IA-SYS-021
      5.2. Electrolyte composition and pH domain UID: IA-SYS-021 STATUS: Active RELATIONS (Parent):
      • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • IA-IRS-007 Electrolyte and hydraulic interface
      • IA-CON-003 Property models
      • IA-ELY-001 Electrolyte ODE
      • IA-TD-010 Electrolyte and loading domain test
      STATEMENT: The default liquid electrolyte SHALL be an aqueous alkaline hydroxide solution.

      The model SHALL accept compositions disclosed in the patents, including:

      - 6 M KOH (US12308414B2; EP4602674A1 simulation electrolyte at 303 K);
      - 5.5 M KOH + 0.5 M LiOH;
      - 6 M NaOH;
      - 5 M NaOH + 1 M KOH;
      - about 4 M KOH + 2 M NaOH + 0.05 M LiOH, or 4 M NaOH + 2 M KOH + 0.05 M LiOH;
      - high hydroxide at or above 5 M, including 5-6 M, 6-7 M, 7-11 M, 7-10 M,
      7.5-9.5 M, and 7 M or greater (EP4602674A1 electrolyte section).

      pH SHALL be modelable as acidic (pH < 4), near-neutral (4 < pH < 10), or alkaline
      (pH > 10), with alkaline pH greater than 10 or greater than 12 as preferred
      operating points (US12308414B2 electrolyte paragraph).

      Electrolyte type MAY also be solid oxide, solid polymer, molten salt, non-aqueous,
      gel, or combinations (EP4602674A1 Claims 14-15), but aqueous alkali hydroxide is
      the verified modeling default.
       RATIONALE: US12308414B2 liquid electrolyte examples; EP4602674A1 Claims 14-15 and [0231]-[0241].
      High hydroxide is disclosed to increase capacity, improve coulombic efficiency, and
      decrease overpotential.
