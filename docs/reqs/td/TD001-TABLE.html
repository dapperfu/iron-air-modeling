        SEC-TD-FOUND
        Foundation and chemistry tests
        TEXT
        These test descriptions specify how phase-gate and patent-mapped requirements are
        executed. Procedures operate on the ironair package. They do not energize physical
        cells from US12308414B2 or EP4602674A1.
        REQUIREMENT
        		1.1
        IA-TD-001
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-001 Python package
        IA-TD-001
        Package import test
        Import ironair and the public subpackages listed in IA-SYS-001.
        No ImportError. __version__ is a nonempty string.
        REQUIREMENT
        		1.2
        IA-TD-002
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-CON-001 Constants module
        IA-TD-002
        Constants immutability test
        Read F_C_MOL, R_J_MOL_K, T_REF_K, P_REF_PA, M_FE_KG_MOL, M_O2_KG_MOL, M_H2O_KG_MOL,
        and M_KOH_KG_MOL. Attempting assignment shall fail.

        Confirm T_REF_K corresponds to 25 C and P_REF_PA to 1 atm per US12308414B2
        standard ambient temperature and pressure.
        Values match documented CODATA/IUPAC constants. Frozen assignment raises.
        REQUIREMENT
        		1.3
        IA-TD-003
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-TST-001 Elemental conservation
        IA-TD-003
        Elemental conservation test
        For each column of S, compute the elemental residual E @ S[:, j] for Fe, O, H, K,
        and C, including Fe/Fe(OH)2, Fe(OH)2/Fe3O4, ORR, OER, and HER columns from
        IA-SYS-020.
        Absolute residual < 1e-12 for Fe, O, H, K, and C.
        REQUIREMENT
        		1.4
        IA-TD-004
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-CHM-007 Butler-Volmer kinetics
        IA-TD-004
        Butler-Volmer equilibrium test
        Evaluate electrode current at zero overpotential with finite activities for iron,
        ORR, OER, and HER couples.
        Net Faradaic current is ~0. Symmetric coefficients produce an odd i(eta) function.

        IA-TD-005
        RHS determinism test
        Evaluate rhs twice with identical arguments for every DynamicComponent, including
        IronElectrode in DRI-bed and channeled modes, OxygenReduction in stacked submerged
        mode, and OxygenEvolution in interdigitated mode.
        Arrays compare equal. Component parameter values are unchanged.

        		2.2

        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-REQ-004 Bidirectional traceability
        IA-TD-006
        Traceability test
        Parse reqs/**/*.sdoc UIDs and src/**/*.py @relation markers.

        Patent-mapped UIDs IA-SYS-006 through IA-SYS-026, IA-CHM-011, IA-SDD-006 through
        IA-SDD-010, IA-IRS-006 through IA-IRS-010, IA-TP-004 through IA-TP-006,
        IA-TD-007 through IA-TD-010, and IA-SCN-PAT-* SHALL exist in StrictDoc.
        Every @relation UID exists. Every implementation requirement listed in the
        traceability map has a function marker.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.3
        IA-TD-007
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-008 Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths
        IA-TD-007
        Dual-electrode switch test
        Place IronAirCell in charge mode and confirm the electrical path is iron-OER-source
        with ORR isolated from OER potential. Switch to discharge and confirm iron-ORR-load
        (EP4602674A1 [0049]; US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
        Mode is discrete. ORR is not held at OER potential during charge when isolation
        is enabled.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.4
        IA-TD-008
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-009 Submerged stacked ORR with depth-equalized pressure drop
        IA-TD-008
        Stacked ORR equal pressure-drop test
        Build a vertical stack of discrete submerged ORR sections with depth-varying
        geometry. Compute algebraic pressure drop in the depth direction
        (US12308414B2 Claim 1).
        Pressure drops compare equal within solver tolerance. Geometry may still vary
        with depth.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.5
        IA-TD-009
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-CHM-011 Coulombic efficiency versus electrode architecture
        IA-TD-009
        Interdigitated coulombic-efficiency trend test
        Run matched planar, channeled, and interdigitated layouts at 6 M KOH, 303 K,
        ChanFrac 0.4, w_rib 5 mm, and 4 g Fe/cm2 (EP4602674A1 FIGS. 19-27 conditions).
        Compare instantaneous coulombic charge efficiency and discharge voltage plateau
        length.
        Interdigitated efficiency is greater than or equal to planar efficiency at
        end-of-charge. Test records architecture flags and does not claim hardware
        validation.
        REQUIREMENT
        		2.6
        IA-TD-010
        Active
        Parents:
        • IA-SYS-021 Electrolyte composition and pH domain
        IA-TD-010
        Electrolyte and loading domain test
        Construct ParameterSets for 6 M KOH, 5.5 M KOH + 0.5 M LiOH, 6 M NaOH, 5 M NaOH +
        1 M KOH, and 7 M hydroxide, and for iron loading 1 and 7 g Fe/cm2 with electrolyte
        volume fraction 0.5 and 0.9 (US12308414B2 electrolyte examples; EP4602674A1
        Claims 16-17). Reject a loading of 0 g Fe/cm2 or a volume fraction of 1.1.
        In-range sets construct. Out-of-range sets raise a domain error.
