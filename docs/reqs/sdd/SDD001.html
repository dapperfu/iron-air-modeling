    SEC-SDD-SRC

    1. Source layout and clean baseline

    UID: SEC-SDD-SRC
    STATEMENT: This Software Design Document (SDD) describes how ironair maps the patented
    iron-air plant onto Python packages, DynamicComponent classes, and algebraic
    versus dynamic state partitions. Physical geometry and chemistry come from
    US12308414B2 (device architectures for metal-air batteries) and EP4602674A1
    (electrode configurations for iron-air electrochemical systems). This SDD does
    not implement a second reaction network besides ironair.chemistry.
    IA-SDD-001
    1.1. Source layout
    UID: IA-SDD-001
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-001 Python package
    STATEMENT: Package sources SHALL live under src/ironair as specified by IA-SYS-001.

    This SDRS src/ layout is the project-specific design and SHALL be used even though
    a generic Cursor rule discourages src/ layouts.

    Patent-derived parameters, architecture enumerations, and claim citations SHALL
    live in typed parameter objects under the same package, not in notebooks.
    COMMENT: pyproject.toml configures setuptools package discovery with where = ["src"].
    IA-SDD-005
    1.2. Clean Python baseline
    UID: IA-SDD-005
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-001 Python package
    STATEMENT: This Python package is a new baseline. It SHALL NOT import, translate, or recover
    governing equations from prior MATLAB or Simulink implementations.

    Patent text SHALL be paraphrased into parameter metadata and requirement
    statements. Governing equations SHALL be implemented from the shared chemistry
    and component protocols, not by copying copyrighted patent prose into source.
    COMMENT: Source tree contains no .m or .slx physics. Chemistry and component modules implement
    the SDRS reaction set and ODE protocol directly.
    SEC-SDD-PROTO

    2. Component protocol and chemistry ownership

    UID: SEC-SDD-PROTO
    IA-SDD-002
    2.1. Dynamic component base
    UID: IA-SDD-002
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-ODE-001 Common component protocol
    STATEMENT: All physical components SHALL implement ironair.ode.protocol.DynamicComponentProtocol
    and MAY inherit ironair.ode.component.DynamicComponent for shared validation.

    rhs(t, y, inputs, context) SHALL be a pure function of its arguments for a fixed
    parameter configuration.

    Architecture flags (floating ORR, stacked submerged ORR, interdigitated OER,
    DRI bed, spiral, pleated) SHALL be parameters, not hidden rhs mutation.
    COMMENT: Protocol checks and unit tests cover every registered component class.
    IA-SDD-003
    2.2. Shared chemistry ownership
    UID: IA-SDD-003
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-004 Shared component models
    STATEMENT: Electrode components SHALL obtain stoichiometric rates from ironair.chemistry rather
    than embedding a second reaction network.

    Iron conversion Fe / Fe(OH)2 / Fe3O4, ORR, OER, and HER SHALL use the capacities
    and half-reactions of IA-SYS-020 (EP4602674A1 [0042]-[0044]; US12308414B2 ORR
    four-electron reaction). Faraday mapping SHALL convert electrode current to molar
    rate.
    COMMENT: Iron, ORR, OER, and HER rate mapping uses ReactionNetwork.S and Faraday conversion.
    IA-SDD-004
    2.3. Algebraic versus dynamic states
    UID: IA-SDD-004
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-003 ODE-based components
    STATEMENT: Instantaneous constitutive laws (Ohmic drop, hydraulic orifice flow at a fixed
    position, Nernst potential, stacked-ORR equal pressure drop of US12308414B2
    Claim 1, Faraday current-to-rate maps) SHALL be algebraic.

    Dynamic states SHALL represent stored mass, charge, thermal energy, momentum, or
    explicit controller memory, including iron phase inventories, electrolyte
    species, gas pockets, HER/OER bubble holdup, and controller modes.

    Artificial lag SHALL NOT be added to algebraic laws.
    COMMENT: CurrentCollector resistance is algebraic. CurrentCollector temperature is dynamic.
    Equalized ORR stack pressure drop is algebraic in geometry and flow.
    SEC-SDD-MAP

    3. Mapping patented architectures onto components

    UID: SEC-SDD-MAP
    STATEMENT: US12308414B2 Claim 1 stacked submerged ORR is IronAirCell plus GasDiffusionLayer
    plus AirSystem with a vertical stack of ORR sections. EP4602674A1 Claim 1
    channeled iron plus interdigitated OER is IronElectrode plus OxygenEvolution with
    channel geometry parameters. Dual-electrode switching is SupervisoryController
    mode plus algebraic electrical connection, not a second chemistry module.
    IA-SDD-006
    3.1. Dual-electrode electrical design
    UID: IA-SDD-006
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-008 Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths
    STATEMENT: IronAirCell SHALL expose separate ORR and OER electrical ports. SupervisoryController
    SHALL connect iron-OER-source in charge and iron-ORR-load in discharge
    (EP4602674A1 [0049]; US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).

    When isolated, ORR potential SHALL NOT be forced to the OER operating potential.
    Bifunctional single-air-electrode mode SHALL be an explicit reduced-order flag.
    RATIONALE: Protects carbon/PTFE ORR layers from OER potentials (US12308414B2). Matches
    EP4602674A1 switch 116.
    IA-SDD-007
    3.2. Iron electrode geometric modes
    UID: IA-SDD-007
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-015 Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER
    STATEMENT: IronElectrode SHALL implement at least these named geometric modes:

    - packed DRI pellet bed (US12308414B2 Claim 18);
    - planar porous electrode (EP4602674A1 FIG. 1 control);
    - channeled rib-and-spine electrode (Claim 1, FIG. 2);
    - discrete anode array (Claim 20);
    - spiral with current collector (Claim 70);
    - pleated electrode (Claim 77).

    Mode-specific parameters SHALL include loading 1-7 g Fe/cm2, electrolyte volume
    fraction 0.5-0.9, channel length/width/spacing, ChanFrac, and spine/rib porosity.
    IA-SDD-008
    3.3. ORR geometric modes
    UID: IA-SDD-008
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-009 Submerged stacked ORR with depth-equalized pressure drop
    STATEMENT: OxygenReduction and GasDiffusionLayer SHALL implement floating, vertical
    natural-air-breathing, inverse box/tube, stacked submerged equal-dP, wavy/rippled,
    helical, and coaxial-core modes listed in IA-SYS-012.

    AirSystem SHALL supply cascading bubbles, regulated chamber pressure, or ambient
    headspace as required by the selected mode. Claim 1 equal pressure drop SHALL be
    an algebraic hydraulic constraint across stacked sections.
    IA-SDD-009
    3.4. Vessel and lid design mapping
    UID: IA-SDD-009
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-011 Stackable vessel and secondary containment
    STATEMENT: BatteryEnclosure SHALL represent inner wall, outer wall, cavity, optional bag-in-box,
    stacking contacts, and can-negative conduction (US12308414B2 Claims 3 and 5;
    FIGS. 1E-1F, 11).

    Lid functions (air distribution, vent, thermal, current collection, sensing, DC
    links) SHALL map to AirSystem, ThermalNetwork, CurrentCollector, DynamicSensor,
    and DCBus ports rather than a monolithic undocumented object.
    IA-SDD-010
    3.5. Spatial discretization of thick electrodes
    UID: IA-SDD-010
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-005 Simulation fidelity
    STATEMENT: When high-fidelity mode is selected, IronElectrode SHALL discretize through-plane
    or in-plane ionic path length so that front-versus-back HER competition of
    EP4602674A1 [0046]-[0047] is representable.

    Interdigitated OER SHALL shorten the modeled ionic path to the entire iron area
    relative to planar OER at the same loading (FIGS. 19-24).
