    IA-SYS-001
    1.1. Python package IA-SYS-001 The software SHALL be implemented as an installable Python package named ironair.

    All public APIs SHALL be importable using:

    import ironair
    from ironair import constants
    from ironair import chemistry
    from ironair import components
    from ironair import simulation
    from ironair import scenarios
    from ironair import plotting

    The implementation SHALL use a standard src/ package layout.

    The project SHALL provide editable installation using:

    pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]"

    • IA-SYS-002
      2.2. Integrated physics simulation IA-SYS-002 The software SHALL model the complete iron-air battery as a coupled multiphysics
      system corresponding to the patented plant.

      The model SHALL include metallic iron electrode, iron hydroxide and oxide phases,
      oxygen reduction electrode, oxygen evolution electrode, hydrogen evolution,
      alkaline electrolyte, electrolyte transport, separator, gas diffusion layer,
      current collectors, cell assembly, thermal system, air intake, fan/blower, gas
      manifolds, ventilation, electrolyte reservoir, circulation pump, piping, valves,
      heat exchanger, battery stack, battery module, battery enclosure, DC electrical
      distribution, DC/DC conversion, grid inverter, transformer, grid interface,
      measurement sensors, battery management, supervisory control, fault management,
      state estimation, and degradation and aging.

      These components SHALL interact through shared physical quantities and conserved
      material, charge, and energy flows.

      Vessel, lid, and air-delivery functions disclosed in US12308414B2 (electrolyte
      containment, secondary containment, thermal management cavity, air distribution,
      process-gas venting, current collection, sensing, DC connections) SHALL map onto
      the enclosure, air-system, thermal, collector, and control components listed
      above.
    IA-SYS-006
    1.2. Patent-grounded plant of record IA-SYS-006 The simulated plant of record SHALL be a rechargeable aqueous alkaline iron-air
    battery with a dual-electrode air side (distinct ORR discharge cathode and OER
    charge cathode), an iron-containing metal electrode, and a liquid electrolyte that
    separates the air-side electrodes from the metal electrode.

    The plant SHALL be consistent with US12308414B2 Claim 1 (ORR, OER, metal electrode,
    and electrolyte) and with EP4602674A1 Claim 1 (iron electrode, ORR electrode with
    air-facing surface, interdigitated OER, and contacting electrolyte).

    Primary-only (ORR-only air electrode) operation MAY be enabled as a documented
    reduced-order mode but SHALL be identified as non-rechargeable relative to the
    secondary dual-electrode plant.

    • IA-SYS-008
      2.3. Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths IA-SYS-008 During charge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the OER
      electrode and a power source so that iron species are reduced toward metallic
      Fe(0).

      During discharge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the
      ORR electrode and a load so that metallic iron is oxidized toward higher-valence
      species including Fe(OH)2 and Fe3O4.

      A mode switch SHALL select charge versus discharge electrical paths as in
      EP4602674A1 [0049] switch 116 and US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B toggle switch 550.

      OER and ORR electrodes SHALL be allowed to be electrically separated so that the
      ORR electrode is not exposed to high OER operating potentials that can damage or
      oxidize the ORR electrode (US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
      • IA-SDD-006
        3.1. Dual-electrode electrical design IA-SDD-006 IronAirCell SHALL expose separate ORR and OER electrical ports. SupervisoryController
        SHALL connect iron-OER-source in charge and iron-ORR-load in discharge
        (EP4602674A1 [0049]; US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).

        When isolated, ORR potential SHALL NOT be forced to the OER operating potential.
        Bifunctional single-air-electrode mode SHALL be an explicit reduced-order flag.
      • IA-SYS-010
        3.2. Separate ORR and OER electrodes IA-SYS-010 The system SHALL model ORR and OER as separate electrodes (US12308414B2 Claim 2)
        and SHALL also support a documented bifunctional single air electrode in which ORR
        and OER are portions of the same electrode (US12308414B2 Summary).

        When separate, both electrodes MAY be submerged (Claim 6).
        • IA-SYS-024
          6.1. Electrode and catalyst materials IA-SYS-024 The metal electrode SHALL be selectable as Li, Na, K, Mg, Ca, Si, Al, Zn, or Fe,
          or alloys substantially of those elements including FeAl, FeZn, and FeMg, with
          iron or iron alloy as the default LODES anode (US12308414B2 anode paragraph).

          Anode physical form SHALL include dense or porous solid, mesh, foam, particles,
          slurry, ink, suspension, paste, and DRI pellet beds.

          ORR gas-diffusion electrodes SHALL support hydrophobic layers with embedded
          conductive carbon, PTFE microporous layers, and carbon-fiber layers. Example
          loadings include an MPL of about 23 wt% PTFE and 77 wt% carbon black and a first
          portion of about 33 wt% PTFE (US12308414B2 cathode construction).

          OER electrodes SHALL support porous metal mesh plus oxygen-evolution catalyst
          including nickel, Ni-Fe alloys, manganese oxide, iron, NiOx, nickel oxyhydroxide,
          iron oxide, and iron oxyhydroxide (EP4602674A1 Claim 11 and catalyst list).
      • IA-CEL-001
        1.9. Complete cell ODE IA-CEL-001 Module ironair.components.cell SHALL provide class IronAirCell.

        The cell SHALL couple iron electrode, air electrode, hydrogen evolution, electrolyte,
        separator, gas diffusion, current collectors, and thermal dynamics.

        The cell SHALL calculate instantaneous terminal voltage, current, power, SOC, and
        available capacity.

        The component SHALL solve coupled nonlinear electrochemical and electrical constraints.

        Charge SHALL close iron to OER to power source; discharge SHALL close iron to ORR
        to load (EP4602674A1 [0049]). Independent charge and discharge length scales
        SHALL be parameterizable (US12308414B2 Lc versus Ld, including 100 h / 300 h
        asymmetric example). Shared-electrolyte baths of multiple cells SHALL be allowed
        (US12308414B2 FIG. 9A vessel 970).

        Discrete modes SHALL include charge (iron-OER-source), discharge (iron-ORR-load),
        rest, protection, and optional airflow-flex isolation of ORR from OER

      3.6. Airflow regulation and ORR separator IA-SYS-014 The air system SHALL include airflow regulation structures (US12308414B2 Claim 14),
      including arrays of ramps configured with holes (Claim 15).

      The ORR electrode SHALL optionally include a porous separator (Claim 17), with
      pore sizes in the 1 micrometer to 1 centimeter range when wrapping stacked-core
      ORR columns (FIG. 36A-36B) to retain gas and limit anode contact with oxygen.

      OER and ORR SHALL be electrically separable by presence or absence of airflow that
      flexes and displaces the ORR electrode (Claim 16).
      • IA-IRS-006
        2.1. Air interface IA-IRS-006 AirSystem and OxygenReduction SHALL exchange named flows for dry air or oxygen,
        humidity, CO2 mole fraction, pO2, volumetric or mass flow, and manifold pressure.

        ORR SHALL expose an electrolyte-facing first surface and an air-facing second
        surface (EP4602674A1 Claim 1). Stacked submerged ORR sections SHALL expose per-

      • IA-GDL-001
        1.5. Gas diffusion ODE IA-GDL-001 Module ironair.components.gas_diffusion SHALL provide class GasDiffusionLayer.

        The component SHALL calculate transient oxygen concentration profiles, effective
        diffusion, pore saturation, flooding, and gas/liquid transport.

        Hydraulic head on vertical ORR SHALL be able to drive liquid migration and
        flooding; OER bubbles trapped at a horizontal air electrode SHALL be able to
        dry the triple-phase boundary (US12308414B2 vertical versus horizontal

    SEC-SRS-ODE

    7. Dynamic modeling rules

     SEC-SRS-ODE
    IA-SYS-003
    7.1. ODE-based components IA-SYS-003 Every physical component SHALL expose a dynamic ODE model.

    Each component SHALL explicitly define: state vector, state units, initial
    conditions, input variables, output variables, governing differential equations,
    algebraic constitutive relations, physical parameters, boundary conditions,
    conservation residuals, numerical constraints, and associated StrictDoc
    requirement IDs.

    Every component SHALL provide a rhs() method returning state derivatives.

    The generic mathematical representation SHALL be:

    dx/dt = f(t, x, inputs, parameters)

    Where required:

    0 = g(t, x, algebraic_variables, inputs, parameters)

    Algebraic variables SHALL be solved consistently during RHS evaluation or through
    a supported DAE backend.

    An artificial time constant SHALL NOT be introduced solely to make an instantaneous
    physical relation appear dynamic.

    Passive structures, such as current collectors, SHALL possess actual dynamic states
    where justified, such as temperature. Their instantaneous electrical resistance
    SHALL remain an algebraic relation.

    Patent-derived algebraic laws SHALL include Ohmic drop, hydraulic orifice and
    equalized stacked-ORR pressure drop, Nernst potential, and Faraday mapping of
    current to reaction rate.
    • IA-SDD-004
      2.3. Algebraic versus dynamic states IA-SDD-004 Instantaneous constitutive laws (Ohmic drop, hydraulic orifice flow at a fixed
      position, Nernst potential, stacked-ORR equal pressure drop of US12308414B2
      Claim 1, Faraday current-to-rate maps) SHALL be algebraic.

      Dynamic states SHALL represent stored mass, charge, thermal energy, momentum, or
      explicit controller memory, including iron phase inventories, electrolyte
      species, gas pockets, HER/OER bubble holdup, and controller modes.

      Artificial lag SHALL NOT be added to algebraic laws.
    • IA-ODE-001
      4.1. Common component protocol IA-ODE-001 Every dynamic component SHALL implement rhs(self, t, y, inputs, context).

      The RHS SHALL be deterministic for a fixed state, time, input, and parameter configuration.

      It SHALL NOT modify component state through hidden side effects.
      • IA-IRS-002
        1.2. RHS signature IA-IRS-002 Every dynamic component SHALL expose:

        def rhs(self, t, y, inputs, context) -> numpy.ndarray

        t is time in seconds, y is a 1-D float64 state vector, inputs is a mapping of named
        physical inputs, and context carries shared couplings without hidden mutation of
        component parameters.
      • IA-SDD-002
        2.1. Dynamic component base IA-SDD-002 All physical components SHALL implement ironair.ode.protocol.DynamicComponentProtocol
        and MAY inherit ironair.ode.component.DynamicComponent for shared validation.

        rhs(t, y, inputs, context) SHALL be a pure function of its arguments for a fixed
        parameter configuration.

        Architecture flags (floating ORR, stacked submerged ORR, interdigitated OER,
        DRI bed, spiral, pleated) SHALL be parameters, not hidden rhs mutation.
      • IA-TD-005
        2.1. RHS determinism test IA-TD-005 Evaluate rhs twice with identical arguments for every DynamicComponent, including
        IronElectrode in DRI-bed and channeled modes, OxygenReduction in stacked submerged
        mode, and OxygenEvolution in interdigitated mode.
