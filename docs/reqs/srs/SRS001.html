    SEC-SRS-PURPOSE

    1. Purpose, sources, and software boundary

    UID: SEC-SRS-PURPOSE
    STATEMENT: This System Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the iron-air-modeling plant
    that the ironair Python package SHALL simulate. Physical architecture, chemistry,
    electrolyte, air-electrode, iron-electrode, vessel, and grid-storage operating
    envelopes are derived from two Form Energy disclosures:

    - US12308414B2, Device architectures for metal-air batteries (granted 2025-05-20;
    Appl. 16/913,877; published family WO2020264344A1 / EP3991234A1 / CN114207915A).
    - EP4602674A1, Electrode configurations for iron-air electrochemical systems
    (published 2025-08-20; Appl. EP23878232.0; WO2024081792A1; priority US 63/379,223).

    The patents overlap as a dual-electrode iron-air family: separate oxygen reduction
    reaction (ORR) and oxygen evolution reaction (OER) positives, an iron-containing
    negative, and alkaline aqueous electrolyte. US12308414B2 emphasizes vessel,
    submerged/stacked ORR, air delivery, and DRI-pellet anodes. EP4602674A1 emphasizes
    channeled/interdigitated iron and OER electrodes for thick, high-areal-capacity
    anodes. Where they differ, both configurations SHALL be modelable.
    STATEMENT: The software is an installable Python package that reconstructs the patented plant
    as coupled ODE/DAE components. Passing tests verifies the software against this
    SRS; it does not constitute experimental hardware validation of US12308414B2 or
    EP4602674A1.

    STATEMENT: The software SHALL be implemented as an installable Python package named ironair.

    All public APIs SHALL be importable using:

    import ironair
    from ironair import constants
    from ironair import chemistry
    from ironair import components
    from ironair import simulation
    from ironair import scenarios
    from ironair import plotting

    The implementation SHALL use a standard src/ package layout.

    The project SHALL provide editable installation using:

    pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]"
    RATIONALE: A single installable package is the integration boundary for notebooks, tests, and
    coupled simulation of the patented iron-air plant. The SDRS src/ layout takes
    precedence over the generic no-src-directory Cursor rule for this project.
    COMMENT: Package ironair installs in a virtual environment. The listed imports succeed after
    pip install -e ".[dev,notebooks]" or the equivalent uv installation.
    IA-SYS-006
    1.2. Patent-grounded plant of record
    UID: IA-SYS-006
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-IRS-010 Scenario and architecture selection interface
    STATEMENT: The simulated plant of record SHALL be a rechargeable aqueous alkaline iron-air
    battery with a dual-electrode air side (distinct ORR discharge cathode and OER
    charge cathode), an iron-containing metal electrode, and a liquid electrolyte that
    separates the air-side electrodes from the metal electrode.

    The plant SHALL be consistent with US12308414B2 Claim 1 (ORR, OER, metal electrode,
    and electrolyte) and with EP4602674A1 Claim 1 (iron electrode, ORR electrode with
    air-facing surface, interdigitated OER, and contacting electrolyte).

    Primary-only (ORR-only air electrode) operation MAY be enabled as a documented
    reduced-order mode but SHALL be identified as non-rechargeable relative to the
    secondary dual-electrode plant.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Summary and FIG. 1A define metal-air cells with anode, cathode,
    electrolyte, and optional gaseous headspace. EP4602674A1 [0048]-[0049] and FIG. 1
    define dual-electrode iron-air cells with switchable charge (OER) and discharge
    (ORR) paths.
    COMMENT: Family note: US12308414B2 is the granted US member of WO2020264344. EP4602674A1 is
    the EP publication of WO2024081792 and is not a literal equivalent of US12308414B2;
    it is a later electrode-geometry disclosure in the same iron-air product family.
    SEC-SRS-MISSION

    2. Mission, duration, and grid context

    UID: SEC-SRS-MISSION
    STATEMENT: Both patents frame iron-air cells as long-duration and ultra-long-duration energy
    storage (LODES) for electric power grids. US12308414B2 Background states that
    existing storage covers milliseconds to hours and that systems of at least 8 h are
    needed. The detailed description defines long/ultra-long duration as 8 h or longer,
    including 8-20 h, 20-24 h, a week, and seasonal (days to months to a year) storage
    of surplus solar generation. EP4602674A1 [0002] restates the greater-than-8 h need.
    IA-SYS-007
    2.1. Long-duration energy storage mission
    UID: IA-SYS-007
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):

    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-ODE-006 Multiscale execution
    • IA-GRD-001 Grid interface ODE

    STATEMENT: The system SHALL model grid-connected LODES operation with energy storage durations
    of 8 hours or longer, including 8 h, 8-20 h, 20 h, 20-24 h, 24 h, week-scale, and
    seasonal (multi-day to multi-month) windows as defined in US12308414B2 (long
    duration / ultra-long duration; LODES).

    Anode thickness and independent charge versus discharge length scales SHALL be
    parameterizable so that example durations disclosed in US12308414B2 can be
    represented: about 3-5 cm anode thickness for about 100 h; about 4-6 cm for about
    300 h; and an asymmetric example of about 100 h charge with about 300 h discharge
    for a 5 cm by 5 cm square anode cross-section.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Definitions and Detailed Description (anode thickness versus duration;
    symmetric versus asymmetric duration). EP4602674A1 [0002] and [0045] motivate thick
    high-areal-capacity iron electrodes to amortize air-electrode cost.
    COMMENT: Default demonstration discharge of 100 h (3.6e5 s) is the short end of the patented
    LODES range, not a claim that hardware has been validated at that duration.

    STATEMENT: The software SHALL model the complete iron-air battery as a coupled multiphysics
    system corresponding to the patented plant.

    The model SHALL include metallic iron electrode, iron hydroxide and oxide phases,
    oxygen reduction electrode, oxygen evolution electrode, hydrogen evolution,
    alkaline electrolyte, electrolyte transport, separator, gas diffusion layer,
    current collectors, cell assembly, thermal system, air intake, fan/blower, gas
    manifolds, ventilation, electrolyte reservoir, circulation pump, piping, valves,
    heat exchanger, battery stack, battery module, battery enclosure, DC electrical
    distribution, DC/DC conversion, grid inverter, transformer, grid interface,
    measurement sensors, battery management, supervisory control, fault management,
    state estimation, and degradation and aging.

    These components SHALL interact through shared physical quantities and conserved
    material, charge, and energy flows.

    Vessel, lid, and air-delivery functions disclosed in US12308414B2 (electrolyte
    containment, secondary containment, thermal management cavity, air distribution,
    process-gas venting, current collection, sensing, DC connections) SHALL map onto
    the enclosure, air-system, thermal, collector, and control components listed
    above.
    RATIONALE: Balance-of-plant and grid interfaces determine operating envelopes of the
    electrochemical cell. US12308414B2 FIGS. 1E-1F, 9A, and 94-102 describe
    multi-function vessels, lids, and bulk energy storage systems. EP4602674A1 FIG. 1
    adds the dual-electrode switch between power source (charge) and load (discharge).
    COMMENT: Every listed component has a DynamicComponent implementation that can be assembled
    into ironair.simulation.CoupledSystem and exchanges named physical flows.
    IA-SYS-008
    2.3. Dual-electrode charge and discharge paths
    UID: IA-SYS-008
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-SDD-006 Dual-electrode electrical design
    • IA-SYS-010 Separate ORR and OER electrodes
    • IA-CEL-001 Complete cell ODE
    • IA-CTL-001 Supervisory control dynamics
    • IA-TD-007 Dual-electrode switch test
    STATEMENT: During charge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the OER
    electrode and a power source so that iron species are reduced toward metallic
    Fe(0).

    During discharge the model SHALL electrically connect the iron electrode to the
    ORR electrode and a load so that metallic iron is oxidized toward higher-valence
    species including Fe(OH)2 and Fe3O4.

    A mode switch SHALL select charge versus discharge electrical paths as in
    EP4602674A1 [0049] switch 116 and US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B toggle switch 550.

    OER and ORR electrodes SHALL be allowed to be electrically separated so that the
    ORR electrode is not exposed to high OER operating potentials that can damage or
    oxidize the ORR electrode (US12308414B2 FIGS. 5A-5B).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 [0048]-[0049] and US12308414B2 dual-cathode architecture. Independent
    OER/ORR materials and geometries are separately optimized (US12308414B2).
    COMMENT: The supervisory controller implements the switch as a discrete operating mode, not
    as a hidden mutation of plant parameters.
    SEC-SRS-US-ARCH

    3. US12308414B2 device architecture (granted claims)

    UID: SEC-SRS-US-ARCH
    STATEMENT: US12308414B2 Claim 1 is the granted independent claim: a battery with ORR electrode,
    OER electrode, metal electrode, and electrolyte separating the air-side electrodes
    from the metal electrode, wherein the ORR electrode is submerged, comprises a
    series of discrete ORR sections stacked vertically below the electrolyte surface,
    each section geometry varies with depth, and pressure drop across each stacked
    section is the same in the depth direction. Claims 2-18 add dual electrodes,
    stackable vessels, wavy/rippled ORR, can-negative housing, inverse-air frames and
    boxes, supplied air, curved/tube ORR, movable metal electrode, airflow ramps,
    airflow-flex electrical separation, porous ORR separator, and DRI pellet beds.
    IA-SYS-009
    3.1. Submerged stacked ORR with depth-equalized pressure drop
    UID: IA-SYS-009
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-SDD-008 ORR geometric modes
    • IA-SCN-PAT-STACKORR Submerged stacked ORR equal pressure drop
    • IA-TD-008 Stacked ORR equal pressure-drop test
    • IA-TP-005 Architecture verification objectives
    STATEMENT: The system SHALL support a submerged ORR electrode comprising a series of discrete
    ORR electrode sections arranged vertically in a stack below the electrolyte
    surface.

    Each discrete ORR section SHALL have geometry that can vary with depth below the
    electrolyte surface.

    The air-path model SHALL be able to impose equal pressure drop across each stacked
    ORR section in the depth direction.

    This configuration SHALL be the default representation of US12308414B2 Claim 1.
    RATIONALE: US12308414B2 Claim 1. Detailed description FIGS. 25A-36B, 57-58, and 35A-35C
    describe stacked-core / distillation-column ORR layers with depth-dependent bubble
    thickness, baffles, and cascading air.
    COMMENT: Equal pressure drop is an algebraic hydraulic constitutive law, not an artificial
    dynamic lag (IA-SYS-003).

    STATEMENT: The enclosure model SHALL represent a stackable vessel that supports ORR, OER,

    (US12308414B2 Claim 3).

    UID: IA-SYS-014

    SEC-SRS-EP-ARCH

    4. EP4602674A1 electrode configurations (published claims)

    IA-SYS-015
    4.1. Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER
    UID: IA-SYS-015
    STATUS: Active

    • IA-SDD-007 Iron electrode geometric modes

    STATEMENT: The system SHALL model an iron electrode in contact with an anode current collector
    and comprising a plurality of channels; an ORR electrode with a first surface
    facing the channels and an opposing second surface in contact with air; an OER
    electrode interdigitated with the channels, with at least a portion of the OER
    disposed within the channels in a direction perpendicular to the ORR plane; and
    electrolyte in contact with the iron electrode, the ORR first surface, the
    channels, and the OER electrode.

    This configuration SHALL be the default representation of EP4602674A1 Claim 1
    (FIG. 2).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 Claim 1 and [0051]-[0058]. Channels carry ionic current while denser
    ribs carry electronic current, decoupling porosity trade-offs ([0050]).

    IA-SYS-018
    4.4. Iron loading, electrolyte volume fraction, and spine/rib porosity
    UID: IA-SYS-018
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-002 Integrated physics simulation
    STATEMENT: Iron electrode areal loading SHALL be parameterizable from 1 g Fe/cm2 to 7 g Fe/cm2
    relative to the direction perpendicular to the ORR electrode (EP4602674A1 Claims 16,
    36, 52, 68, 75, 84).

    Electrolyte volume fraction in the iron electrode SHALL be parameterizable from
    0.5 to 0.9 based on total electrode volume when fully charged (Claims 17, 37, 53,
    69, 76, 85), measurable in hardware by mercury porosimetry.

    Spine portions connecting ribs MAY have electrolyte volume fraction 2-20 vol%
    (example 5-15 vol%) lower than ribs, spine thickness 10-50% of rib thickness, and
    spine porosity at least 5 vol% lower than ribs, or alternatively spine and rib
    porosity within plus or minus 5% ([0059]-[0061]).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 Claims 16-17 and [0059]-[0061]. Higher loading amortizes air-electrode
    cost ([0045]) but planar electrodes lose coulombic efficiency as thickness grows
    ([0046]-[0047], FIGS. 23-24).
    IA-SYS-019
    4.5. Electrode assembly manufacturing sequence
    UID: IA-SYS-019
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • IA-SYS-015 Channeled iron electrode with interdigitated OER
    STATEMENT: A documented assembly scenario SHALL form iron negative material onto an anode
    current collector to create channels, dispose OER into one or more channels, and
    assemble ORR with its first surface facing the iron electrode and second surface
    in contact with air (EP4602674A1 Claim 86).

    Forming MAY include compressing the iron material onto the current collector
    (Claim 87) and sintering (Claim 88), followed by adding electrolyte (Claim 89).
    DRI pellet beds MAY alternatively be compressed into an anode-current-collector
    assembly using passive mechanical locking or threaded rods (US12308414B2
    FIGS. 92-93).
    RATIONALE: EP4602674A1 Claims 86-89; US12308414B2 FIGS. 92-93. These steps define initial
    porosity, contact resistance, and channel geometry states.

    SEC-SRS-ODE

    7. Dynamic modeling rules

    UID: SEC-SRS-ODE
    IA-SYS-003
    7.1. ODE-based components
    UID: IA-SYS-003
    STATUS: Active
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • IA-SDD-004 Algebraic versus dynamic states
    • IA-ODE-001 Common component protocol
    • IA-FE-001 Iron electrode ODE
    STATEMENT: Every physical component SHALL expose a dynamic ODE model.

    Each component SHALL explicitly define: state vector, state units, initial
    conditions, input variables, output variables, governing differential equations,
    algebraic constitutive relations, physical parameters, boundary conditions,
    conservation residuals, numerical constraints, and associated StrictDoc
    requirement IDs.

    Every component SHALL provide a rhs() method returning state derivatives.

    The generic mathematical representation SHALL be:

    dx/dt = f(t, x, inputs, parameters)

    Where required:

    0 = g(t, x, algebraic_variables, inputs, parameters)

    Algebraic variables SHALL be solved consistently during RHS evaluation or through
    a supported DAE backend.

    An artificial time constant SHALL NOT be introduced solely to make an instantaneous
    physical relation appear dynamic.

    Passive structures, such as current collectors, SHALL possess actual dynamic states
    where justified, such as temperature. Their instantaneous electrical resistance
    SHALL remain an algebraic relation.

    Patent-derived algebraic laws SHALL include Ohmic drop, hydraulic orifice and
    equalized stacked-ORR pressure drop, Nernst potential, and Faraday mapping of
    current to reaction rate.
    COMMENT: Every DynamicComponent publishes StateSpec metadata and a deterministic
    rhs(t, y, inputs, context). Algebraic electrical resistance is not converted into
    a fake lag state.
